'''''Minecraft: Steve Rocks the Block (Original Soundtrack)''''' 是为[[任天堂明星大乱斗特别版]]的Challenger Pack 7创作的迷你专辑，包含了多首音乐的混音版本。它由Johan Johnson、[[Peter Hont]]、[[Gareth Coker]]和Shauny Jang作曲，由ACE（TOMori Kudo / CHiCO）、{{w|Yoko Shimomura}}、Mitsuhiro Kitadani、Yuzo Koshiro、Hiroki Hashimoto和Motoi Sakuraba混音。该专辑于2026年2月10日发布，但最初于1月26日至1月31日在流媒体服务上提供，后被关闭。<ref>{{reddit|1qo011d/minecraft_steve_rocks_the_block_original||Minecraft: Steve Rocks the Block (Original Soundtrack)|Semario205|January 27, 2026}}</ref>{{better source|This Reddit post proves the EP was out by January 27, though it does not prove it was released on January 26 and taken down on January 31.}}

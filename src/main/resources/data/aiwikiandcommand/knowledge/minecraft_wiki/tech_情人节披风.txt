'''情人节披风（Valentine Cape）'''是赠送给两名玩家的个人披风。

== 描述 ==
这件披风是由Mojang员工Ekkelangelo赠送给LolFoxy2的，Ekkelangelo帮助LolFoxy2解决了备用账户的迁移问题。<ref>{{Citation|url=https://laby.net/cape/bfa8cc9d7af57fd856890af7c2bf2a4a|title=This cape was given to @LolFoxy2 by the Mojang employee @Ekkelangelo after resolving LolFoxy2's problems with Migration.|website=laby.net}}</ref><ref>https://i.imgur.com/ZgROuy8.png</ref>但二十分钟后被迅速移除。

它也被赠送给了玩家JoinGrave，可能是出于意外，随后在两天后被移除。<ref>{{Citation|url=https://laby.net/cape/bfa8cc9d7af57fd856890af7c2bf2a4a|title=On the 24th of January 2023 it was also discovered for a short time on the profile @JoinGrave.|website=laby.net}}</ref>

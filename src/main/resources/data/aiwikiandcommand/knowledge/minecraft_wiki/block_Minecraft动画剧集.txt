'''Minecraft动画剧集'''是一部基于同名游戏[[Minecraft]]改编的CG动画剧集，由[[Mojang Studios]]、[[wikipedia:WildBrain Studios|WildBrain Studios]]与[[wikipedia:Flying Bark Productions|Flying Bark Productions]]联合制作，将在[[wzh:Netflix|Netflix]]独家上线。<ref name=mcnetarticle>{{Mcnet|netflix-series|Netflix series|May 30, 2024|Per Landin}}</ref>

== 剧情 ==
Netflix称，该剧“将创作出原创剧情和新角色，以新的视角展示Minecraft的世界观。” <ref>[https://variety.com/2024/tv/news/minecraft-animated-series-netflix-1236019300/ 'Minecraft' Animated Series is in the Works at Netflix]</ref><ref>[https://deadline.com/2024/05/minecraft-animated-series-netflix-new-characters-1235944553/ 'Minecraft' Animated Series Set At Netflix With New Characters]</ref>

== 制作 ==
2024年5月30日，Netflix发布预告片，宣布制作以Minecraft为主题的动画{{tr|剧集|影集}}。 由制作过《[[wikipedia:Sonic Prime|刺猬索尼克系列——音速小子：回家大冒险]]》、《[[wikipedia:Ninjago: Dragons Rising|{{tr|幻影忍者：神龙崛起|旋風忍者：猛龍崛起|旋風忍者：龍之崛起}}]]》和《[[wzh:Carmen Sandiego (TV series)|Carmen Sandiego]]》的WildBrain Studios负责CG动画，Netflix和Mojang Studios负责剧情和选角。<ref name=mcnetarticle/>

2025年6月，Netflix在[[wikipedia:2025 Annecy International Animation Film Festival|2025年安纳西国际动画电影节]]分享了剧集的最新动态，表示剧集将采用精细的动画制作，“既忠于原作游戏，又将带来全新的冒险，为粉丝打造一场全新的体验”。 同时公布剧集主管为Kevin Adams与Joe Ksander，[[wikipedia:A. C. Bradley (screenwriter)|A. C. Bradley]]担任首席编剧。<ref>{{tweet|NexusPointNews|1933167455234322587|‘MINECRAFT’ animated series is in the works at Netflix.<br><br>— The series has detailed animation.<br>— It will be authentic to the game while offering new adventures.|June 12, 2025}}</ref><ref>{{citation|url=https://variety.com/2025/tv/global/minecraft-clash-of-clans-magic-the-gathering-netflix-1236429388/|title=‘Minecraft,’ ‘Clash of Clans,’ ‘Magic the Gathering’ and ‘Ghostbusters’ Series Teased at Netflix Annecy Studio Focus|section=“Minecraft”|website=[https://variety.com/ Variety]|author=Jamie Lang|date=June 12, 2025}}</ref><ref>{{citation|url=https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/news/everything-netflix-announced-and-showcased-at-annecy-2025/|title=Every Animated Movie and Series Netflix Announced and Showcased at Annecy 2025|section=Family Spectacle|website=[https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/ What's on Netflix]|author=Kasey Moore|date=June 12, 2025|archive-url}}</ref>

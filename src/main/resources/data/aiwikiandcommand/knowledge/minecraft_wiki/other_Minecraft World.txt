'''Minecraft World'''是[[Mojang Studios]]与{{w|默林娱乐}}合作推出的Minecraft主题乐园，预计在2027年于英国{{Wikipedia|en:Chessington World of Adventures|切斯顿冒险世界主题公园}}内推出，2026至2027年于美国推出。默林娱乐表示该项目将逐步扩展至全球更多地点。<ref>{{citation|url=https://trademarks.justia.com/990/07/minecraft-99007845.html|title=MINECRAFT ADVENTURE - Trademark Details|website=justia.com}}</ref>

默林娱乐在2024年11月19日宣布了本项目。<ref name="announcement">{{citation|url=https://www.merlinentertainments.biz/newsroom/news-releases/2024/merlin-minecraft-in-real-life/|title=Minecraft In Real Life: Merlin Entertainments And Mojang Studios Join Forces To Create Location-Based Attractions|website=merlinentertainments.biz|date=November 19, 2024}}</ref><ref>{{tweet|MerlinEntsNews|1858798510981734786|We're teaming up with Mojang Studios and @Minecraft for a global partnership that will see adventures brought to life like never before through immersive and interactive experiences 🎮👾<br><br>Are you ready to play? ➡️ merlinentertainments.biz/newsroom/news-…|November 19, 2024}}</ref>[[Mojang Studios]]在[[Minecraft LIVE – March 2026]]中公布了项目的详细信息。

== 概览 ==
该合作旨在将Minecraft冒险体验带入现实世界，在全球各地打造沉浸式互动娱乐景点，包括Minecraft主题游乐设施、数字现实体验、客房住宿、零售餐饮等。<ref name="announcement" />

已确认的英国乐园内将包含多种娱乐设施、互动冒险、积木游乐场、主题零售与餐饮，包括全球最大的Minecraft主题商店与全球首个Minecraft主题过山车。还邀请DanTDM、LDShadowLady、Aimsey、Grian等多名YouTuber参与共建。<ref>{{citation|url=https://www.chessington.com/minecraft/|title=Minecraft World|website=Chessington.com}}</ref>

'''Play Mechanix'''是一家美国坐落于[[wzh:斯科基 (伊利諾伊州)|伊利诺斯州斯科基村]]的[[wzh:街机|街机游戏]]开发商，著名作品有《[[wikipedia:Big Buck Hunter|大雄鹿猎人]]》等。此公司与[[Mojang Studios]]共同负责开发[[Dungeons:街机版|Minecraft Dungeons Arcade]]。

== 开发游戏 ==
Play Mechanix开发了下列街机游戏：<ref>https://rawthrills.com/games/games-archive/</ref><ref>https://rawthrills.com/games/</ref><ref>https://rawthrills.com/tag/play-mechanix/</ref>:

* ''{{w|Big Buck Hunter}}'' (2000)
* ''Big Buck Hunter: Shooter's Challenge'' (2002)
* ''Big Buck Hunter II: Sportman's Paradise'' (2003)
* ''Johnny Nero: Action Hero'' (2004)
* ''Big Buck Hunter Pro'' (2005)
* ''Big Buck Hunter 2006: Call of the Wild'' (2006)
* ''Aliens: Extermination'' (2008)
* ''Big Buck Safari'' (2008)
* ''Wheel of Fortune'' (2009)
* ''Big Buck Hunter Pro: Open Season'' (2010)
* ''Terminator: Salvation'' (2010)
* ''Big Buck World'' (2010)
* ''Big Buck HD'' (2012)
* ''Aliens: Armageddon'' (2013)
* ''Barrel of Monkeys'' (2013)
* ''Big Buck HD Wild'' (2015)
* ''MotoGP'' (2015)
* ''Angry Birds Arcade'' (2016)
* ''The Walking Dead'' (2017)
* ''Halo: Fireteam Raven'' (2018)
* ''Big Buck Hunter: Reloaded'' (2019)
* ''Nitro Trucks'' (2020)
* ''[[Dungeons:街机版|Minecraft Dungeons Arcade]]'' (2021)

同时也开发了下列电子游戏：

* ''Big Buck Hunter: Arcade'' (PC/PS4/ONE/NSwitch, 2016-2017)

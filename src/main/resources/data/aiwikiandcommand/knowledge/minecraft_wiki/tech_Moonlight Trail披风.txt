'''Moonlight Trail披风（Moonlight Trail Cape）'''是发放给参加[[Minecraft Experience: Moonlight Trail]]玩家的披风。<ref name=":0">{{cite|url=https://www.minecraftexperience.com/moonlight-trail-argentina/en/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260514220420/https://www.minecraftexperience.com/moonlight-trail-argentina/en/|title=Minecraft Experience Moonlight Trail Argentina|website=minecraftexperience.com}}</ref>

== 外观 ==
披风的设计以一个钻石[[信标金字塔]]为中心，信标发出的光柱照射在天空中的月亮上。

== 描述 ==
玩家参与Minecraft Experience: Moonlight Trail即可[https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/redeem 兑换奖励代码]，获得这件披风。代码必须在活动结束后30天内兑换，兑换地点为获得代码的巡回城市。<ref name="MC Experience Terms">https://www.minecraftexperience.com/terms/?ref=moonlight-trail-argentina&cp_landing_source=www.minecraftexperience&utm_source=direct</ref>

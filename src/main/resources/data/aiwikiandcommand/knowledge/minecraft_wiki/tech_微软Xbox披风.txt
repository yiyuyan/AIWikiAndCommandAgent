'''微软Xbox披风（Microsoft Xbox Cape）'''是于{{el|Xbox 360}}发放给Microsoft员工的披风。

== 描述 ==
这件披风发放给了微软员工，他们可在Xbox账户中设置该披风的可见性。<ref>{{tweet|PopsRamjet|1128979747382452224|archive-rev=20220326205242|#minecraftmemories Time to pull another photo of out the archives :) Once upon a time we used to have the ability to turn capes on & off on in #MinecraftXBLA #MinecraftX360 This is the cape that Microsoft folks had when they played online! @mojang had one too & our 1st birthday!|May 16, 2019}}</ref>

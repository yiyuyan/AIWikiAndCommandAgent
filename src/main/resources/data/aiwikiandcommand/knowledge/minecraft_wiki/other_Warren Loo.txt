'''沃伦·“EvilSeph”·卢（Warren 'EvilSeph' Loo）'''曾经是一名为[[Mojang Studios]]工作的游戏开发者，亦是前[[Bukkit]]团队管理者。Loo受雇于Mojang以维护[[Minecraft]]服务器并开发官方[[插件API]]。然而在这些完成之前，他的工作领域扩大了，包括日常游戏开发。他在2013年10月2日宣布从Mojang辞职，表示他将会找到一些其他感兴趣的东西。<ref>{{tweet|EvilSeph|385537792794959872}}</ref>

2014年8月21日，他宣布Bukkit项目由于未公开的原因中止，理由是Minecraft EULA的更改以及Mojang的支持不够。<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20140821103657/http://forums.bukkit.org/threads/bukkit-its-time-to-say.305106/</ref> 此决定后来被声称拥有此项目的Mojang推翻。<ref>{{tweet|jeb_|502380018216206336|Warren over at bukkit seems to have forgotten that the project was bought by Mojang over two years ago, and isn't his to discontinue.|August 21, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{tweet|Dinnerbone|502389963606867968|To make this clear: Mojang owns Bukkit. I'm personally going to update Bukkit to 1.8 myself. Bukkit IS NOT and WILL NOT BE the official API.|August 21, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{tweet|_grum|502381523241144320|We took ownership of the Bukkit github repos & project. We'll see what happens from here.|August 21, 2014}}</ref>

'''1.21.132'''是'''[[与铜携手，合作共争]]'''的正式版，也是{{el|ee}}的一次次要更新，发布于2026年2月17日<ref>{{cite|url=https://edusupport.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360047556451-Minecraft-Education-Change-Log|title=Minecraft Education Change Log|website=Minecraft Education Support}}</ref><ref>{{cite|url=https://edusupport.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/46369414349076-What-s-New-in-the-Copper-Collaboration-Compete-Update-v1-21-132|title=What's New in the Copper, Collaboration & Compete Update (v1.21.132)|website=Minecraft Education Support}}</ref>。此次更新加入了[[铜器时代]]和[[群骑纷争]]的特性和专用服务器。

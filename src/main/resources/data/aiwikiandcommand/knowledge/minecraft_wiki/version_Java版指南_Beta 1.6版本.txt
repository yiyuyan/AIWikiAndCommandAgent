{|{{Simple Guide table|Changed Blocks}}
|-
|[[床]]
|[[File:Red Bed (N) JE1.png|84px]]
|

|-

|[[File:Fire JE1.png|84px]]
|

|{{Animate|Brown Mushroom JE1 BE1.png;Red Mushroom JE1 BE1.png|84px}}

|{{Animate|Powered Rail (NS) JE1 BE1.png;Powered Powered Rail (NS) JE1 BE1.png|84px}}

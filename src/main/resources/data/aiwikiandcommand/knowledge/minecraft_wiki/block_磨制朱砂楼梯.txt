'''磨制朱砂楼梯（Polished Cinnabar Stairs）'''是[[磨制朱砂]]制作的[[楼梯]]。

== 生成 ==
磨制朱砂楼梯不会生成在世界中。

== 获取 ==
=== 合成 ===
{{Crafting
|A1= Polished Cinnabar |A2= Polished Cinnabar |A3= Polished Cinnabar
|B2= Polished Cinnabar |B3= Polished Cinnabar
|C3= Polished Cinnabar
|Output= Polished Cinnabar Stairs,4
|type= Building block
}}

=== 切石 ===
{{Stonecutting
|Cinnabar; Polished Cinnabar
|Polished Cinnabar Stairs
}}

== 破坏 ==
挖掘磨制朱砂楼梯的合适工具是镐。磨制朱砂楼梯需要木镐或品质更好的镐来挖掘，否则挖掘时间将延长且不会掉落。

{{Breaking table}}

磨制朱砂楼梯被破坏后会掉落自身。

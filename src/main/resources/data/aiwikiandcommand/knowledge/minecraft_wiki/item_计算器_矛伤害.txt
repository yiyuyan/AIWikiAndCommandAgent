此互動式計算機可以計算給定速度下玩家使用[[矛]]冲锋攻击能造成的傷害（已包含玩家的1点基础伤害），單位為格每秒。

  "Java版": 1,
  "基岩版": 0
} |default=1}}</div>
</div>
<div style="display: flex; flex-direction: row; align-items: center; gap: 1rem;">

  "木矛": 1,
  "金矛": 2,
  "石矛": 3,
  "{{lan|zh=銅矛|zh-cn=铜矛}}": 4,

} |default=5}}{{simplecalc|id=multiplier|type=hidden|formula=switch(tier, 1,0.7, 2,0.7, 3,0.82, 4,0.82, 5,0.95, 6,1.075, 7,1.2)|default=0.95}}{{simplecalc|id=base|type=hidden|default=1}}</div>
</div>
<div style="display: flex; flex-direction: row; align-items: center; gap: 1rem;">
{{simplecalc label|速度（m/s）：|for=speed}} <div class="cdx-text-input">{{simplecalc|id=speed|type=number|default=5.612|min=4.6|step=0.1|decimals=2|class-live=cdx-text-input__input|formula=speedp}}</div>
</div>
<div style="display: flex; flex-direction: row; align-items: center; gap: 1rem;">
{{simplecalc label|[[锋利]]等级：|for=sharpness}} <div class="cdx-text-input">{{simplecalc|id=sharpness|type=number|default=0|min=0|max=5|class-live=cdx-text-input__input}}{{simplecalc|id=sharpbonus|type=hidden|formula=(edition*(sign(sharpness)+sharpness)/2)+((1-edition)*floor(1.25*sharpness))|default=0}}</div>
</div>
<div style="display: flex; flex-direction: row; align-items: center; gap: 1rem;">
{{simplecalc label|造成傷害：|for=damage}} <div class="cdx-text-input">{{simplecalc|id=damage|type=number|default=6|min=0|class-live=cdx-text-input__input|formula=ifgreaterorequal(speed, 4.2, base+sharpbonus+floor(speed*multiplier), 0)}}{{simplecalc|id=speedp|type=hidden|formula=ifequal(damage, base+sharpbonus+floor((speed+0.01)*multiplier), speed, max(ceil(damage-base-sharpbonus)/multiplier, 4.6))}}</div>

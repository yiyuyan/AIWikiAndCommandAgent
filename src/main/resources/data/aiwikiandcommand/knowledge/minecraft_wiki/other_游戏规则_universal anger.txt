'''universal_anger'''是控制[[生物#条件敌对|条件敌对生物]]被激怒时行为的[[游戏规则]]。

== 行为 ==
当universal_anger为<samp>true</samp>时，[[僵尸猪灵]]、[[末影人]]、[[北极熊]]、[[猪灵]]、[[狼]]、[[蜜蜂]]和[[铁傀儡]]被激怒时会对附近的所有[[玩家]]敌对，而不再限于激怒它们的玩家。

无论此游戏规则为何值，[[蜜蜂]]所在[[蜂巢（方块）|蜂巢]]被破坏时仍然会攻击附近所有玩家。<ref>{{tweet|henrikkniberg|1268765029777772549|Revamped anger management for neutral mobs in minecraft. Now angered neutral mobs won't attack innocent bystanders, won't forget you just cuz you log out & log in, and won't keep killing you after you die & respawn. Etc. Find anything wonky? Report at bugs.mojang.com|2020年6月5日}}</ref>

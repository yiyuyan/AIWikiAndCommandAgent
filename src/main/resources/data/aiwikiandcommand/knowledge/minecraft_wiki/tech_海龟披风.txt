'''海龟披风（Turtle Cape）'''是一件个人披风。

== 描述 ==
billyK_因建议在游戏中加入[[海龟]]而被给予该披风。<ref>[https://www.reddit.com/r/Minecraft/comments/7veimo/turtle_shoutout_to_billyk_as_suspected_theres_a/ Turtle shoutout to billyK_! As suspected, there's a cape waiting for you on your Minecraft profile =)]</ref>
该披风又因为端点测试而被给予了Mojang雇员[https://namemc.com/profile/fe5d6f18-6b24-4afb-8fba-fd8768d96e4b Michael Andersson]和[https://namemc.com/profile/e9cd1af9-6152-40d7-b957-4969094e077c el_hefe_]，但el_hefe_的披风之后被移除了。

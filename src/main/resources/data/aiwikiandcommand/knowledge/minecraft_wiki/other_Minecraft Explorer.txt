[[Minecraft Explorer]]是由[[57Digital]]开发的官方Minecraft配套指南应用程序<ref>{{citation|url=https://mcupdate.tumblr.com/post/31269640713/supporting-minecraft-apps-on-ios|title=Supporting Minecraft Apps on iOS|website=
McUpdate}}</ref><ref>{{tweet|carlmanneh|182406087545339904|Need a companion app for Minecraft? We recommend this one: http://itunes.apple.com/ng/app/minecraft-explorer-pro/id449354665?mt{{=}}8 …|March 21, 2012}}</ref>。用户可以通过该应用查询Minecraft中的[[合成配方]]、[[物品]]、[[方块]]和[[生物]]等信息。

== 购买和可用性 ==
[https://apps.apple.com/us/app/minecraft-explorer-pro/id449354665 Minecraft Explorer Pro]可以在{{w|App Store}}上以{{price|2.99}}的价格购买。

[https://web.archive.org/web/20210729223621/https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id514593643 Minecraft Explorer Pro HD]可以在App Store上以{{price|3.99}}的价格购买。

[https://web.archive.org/web/20150413200731/https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/minecraft-explorer-lite/id430384234?Mt=8 Minecraft Explorer Lite]可以在App Store上免费下载。

该应用程序仅在{{w|iOS}}平台上可用。

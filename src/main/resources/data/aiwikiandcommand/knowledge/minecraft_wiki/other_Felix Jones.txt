'''Felix Jones'''，也被称作'''Xilefian'''（{{Audio|XilefianPronunciation.mp3| /ˈzaɪleˌfiːən/ |link2=''ZY-leh-fee-ən''}}）<ref name=":0">{{tweet|Xilefian|1370783984947445767|Someone stick this on my Minecraft wiki page please.<br>Xilefian /ˈzaɪleˌfiːən/|March 13, 2021}}</ref>，在[[Mojang Studios]]担任技术主管。

== 关于 ==
2020年1月，Felix加入Mojang担任游戏开发者，负责Java版游戏开发。<ref name=":1">{{tweet|Xilefian|1202640572399394817|This January I'm joining Mojang and the Minecraft team as a Java Game Developer! I remember the first day I played Minecraft in Alpha and now - all these years later - I have the chance to contribute back to a landmark title that changed the world. Will Tweet more! #Minecraft|December 5, 2019}}</ref>

2022年12月，Felix晋升为技术主管。

== 你知道吗 ==
* Felix Jones从[[Java版Alpha|Alpha]]版就开始玩Minecraft了。<ref name=":1" />
* Felix Jones曾在决定如何给他的Minecraft[[皮肤]]添加眼睛之后狂笑了45分钟。<ref>{{tweet|Xilefian|1475816459330899968|I spent about 45 minutes laughing maniacally after deciding how to do the eyes on my Minecraft skin. Reject conformity; embrace chaos. You can put that on my Minecraft wiki page|2021年12月28日}}</ref><!-- 这段“你知道吗”是被Felix本人推荐记载在Minecraft Wiki里的，因此不应当被移除 -->
* [[Java版1.16-rc1]]中加入“#minecraftfarms”[[闪烁标语]]是因为Felix Jones试图让这个话题进入推特动态趋势榜。<ref>{{tweet|Xilefian|1270028640340914178|#minecraftfarms|2020年6月8日}}; {{tweet|Xilefian|1270032089342259202|just trying to get the hashtag trending|2020年6月8日}}</ref>

'''科-{里}-·克莱曼斯（Corey Clemans）'''曾经是[[Mojang AB|Minecraft]]的拉丁美洲社区协调专员。2018年7月16日，他宣布离开Mojang。<ref>{{tweet|CoreyClemans|1018887566299299840|Hello, my friends. The time is quickly approaching for when I must say goodbye to you all. I have decided to take an opportunity outside of the videogame industry. I have greatly enjoyed getting to know each of you and I’m sure we’ll see each other in Minecraft. All the best!|July 16, 2018}}</ref>

== 关于 ==
Corey当前的职务包括协助Minecraft社区管理特许权和专职于拉丁美洲社区的管理。

== 你知道吗 ==
* Corey在Microsoft Studios Community Team时和[[Helen Zbihlyj]]在一起工作。
* Corey的直播以西班牙语、葡萄牙语和英语进行。
* 他最爱的颜色是绿色。

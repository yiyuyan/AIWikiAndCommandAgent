'''格奥尔基·“Fry”·加夫-{里}-切夫（Georgii 'Fry' Gavrichev）'''是[[Mojang Studios]]的一位游戏开发者，致力于Minecraft Java版的相关工作。他于2017年10月6日加入Java版开发团队。<ref>{{tweet|jeb|916229857255845888|Shoutout for our most recent Minecraft Java Edition developer @fry_su ! Он ответит на все ваши русские вопросы;)|October 6, 2017}}，''翻译：“他会回答你们俄语的问题。”''</ref>

他因解决技术性的照明问题<ref>{{bug|MC-148723|||Fixed}}</ref>、维护Data Fixer Upper<ref>https://github.com/Mojang/DataFixerUpper/graphs/contributors</ref>和从事Blaze 3D<ref>{{tweet|SeargeDP|1127929965704622081|As some of you guessed correctly, the last image I posted was about Blaze 3D. An older branch that was parked for a while has been successfully rebased and @fry_su and I will resume work on it soon. There's a lot of work ahead of us, so we'll stick to "when it's done" for now.}}</ref>相关的工作而闻名。

在他加入Mojang Studios之前，他曾因致力于模组[[模型]]的API相关工作而广为人知。<ref>https://github.com/MinecraftForge/MinecraftForge/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3ARainWarrior+is%3Aclosed</ref>

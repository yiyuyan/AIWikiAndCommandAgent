'''Minecraft x Higround'''是[[Minecraft]]与Higround合作推出的一系列周边商品。<ref>{{tweet|Minecraft|1793337473713750328|Like crafting and building within Minecraft, this collection celebrates 15 years of the Minecraft community’s creative spirit that’s lives beyond the game. Shape your world with @Higround x Minecraft ⛏⌨ #Minecraft15 Limited Drop 5/29 12PM PT: https://aka.ms/MinecraftHiground|May 21, 2024}}</ref><ref>{{mcnet|minecraft-x-higround|Minecraft x Higround|May 22, 2024|Cristina Aderca}}</ref>于[[15周年庆]]期间公布，并于太平洋时间（UTC-8）2024年5月29日中午12时限量发售。

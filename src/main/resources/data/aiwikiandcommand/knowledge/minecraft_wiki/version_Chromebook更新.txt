If you're planning on using #MinecraftEdu on August 6 or 7, make sure all devices are updated to avoid any disruptions.|28 July 2020}}</ref><ref name="1.14.31">{{tweet|PlayCraftLearn|1291511878137651202|A reminder to our community: We have an update scheduled for today, so make sure your classroom or student devices have the latest version of #MinecraftEdu to avoid any disruptions. {{main|基岩版1.13.0|基岩版1.14.0}}
* 加入了[[基岩版1.13.0]]和[[基岩版1.14.0|1.14.0]]的特性。

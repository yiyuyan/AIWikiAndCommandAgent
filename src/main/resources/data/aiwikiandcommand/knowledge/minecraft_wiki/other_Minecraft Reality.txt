'''Minecraft Reality'''是由[[:en:13th Lab|13th Lab]]开发的付费增强现实（AR）应用程序，发布于2012年11月25日<ref name=:0>{{cite|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121127183203/http://mojang.com/2012/11/announcing-minecraft-reality-for-ios/|title=Announcing Minecraft Reality for iOS|author=Owen|date=November 25, 2012|website=Mojang.com}}</ref>，并在2015年初宣布终止支持。

== 购买和可用性 ==
Minecraft Reality以{{price|1.99}}的价格在{{w|App Store (iOS/iPadOS)|App Store}}出售。<ref name=:0/><ref name="theverge">{{citation|url=https://www.theverge.com/2012/11/25/3688674/minecraft-augmented-reality-iphone-app|title=Minecraft Reality lets you see your virtual creations in the real world|website=The Verge|author=Andrew Webster|date=November 25, 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/0/https://www.theverge.com/2012/11/25/3688674/minecraft-augmented-reality-iphone-app}}</ref>2015年初，该应用不再能购买，这可能是由于它的开发商13th Lab在这一年3月与{{w|Oculus}}/{{w|Meta Platforms|Meta}}合并<ref>{{cite|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150411164744/http://13thlab.com/|title=We’re joining Oculus!|website=13thlab.com}}</ref>。

该应用仅支持在{{w|iPhone}}、{{w|iPad}}和{{w|iPod Touch}}上运行。<ref name=:1>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140816130454/https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/minecraft-reality/id577991556?ls=1&mt=8|title=Minecraft Reality on the App Store on iTunes|website=web.archive.org}}</ref>

== 内容 ==
Minecraft Reailty允许用户使用其网站在应用程序中上传自己创作的Minecraft内容。用户上传的内容可以在应用程序中找到。使用“Place a creation”按钮，该应用会使用设备的相机将3D模型放入现实世界。 此外，玩家可以使用应用程序底部中角的相机按钮共享屏幕截图。

== 开发 ==
Minecraft Reality利用了“[https://web.archive.org/web/0/http://developer.pointcloud.io/sdk PointCloud SDK]”库的功能，以及SLAM（同步本地化和映射）来跟踪3D空间。<ref>{{cite|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121127121544/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2pOpcR7uf5U|title=Minecraft Reality|author=13thLab|website=YouTube|quote=Powered by PointCloud SDK (http<nowiki/>://pointcloud.io) which lets you map and track 3D spaces using SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping).}}</ref><ref>{{cite|url=https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/bring-minecraft-creations-life-augmented-reality-app-flna1C7289186|title=Bring 'Minecraft' creations to life with augmented-reality app|website=NBC News|author=Devin Coldewey|date=November 27, 2012}}</ref>这两种技术都是由13th Lab开发的。

== 系统需求 ==
Minecraft Reality要求iOS 5.0或更高版本、iPhone 4或更高版本、iPad 2 Wi-Fi或更高版本，或iPod touch（第4代）或更高版本。<ref name=:1/>

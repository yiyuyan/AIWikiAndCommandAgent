'''乔什·“rockenroll4life”·莱特利尔（Josh 'rockenroll4life' Letellier）'''曾经自2018年7月16日为[[Mojang Studios]]所属的[[Java版|Minecraft：Java版]]开发团队的一员。<ref>{{Tweet|rockenroll4life|1016896738760671232}}</ref><ref>{{Tweet|rockenroll4life|1018755187249381377}}</ref>在此之前，他在Minecraft雷德蒙德办公处工作。

他也拥有YouTube账户[https://www.youtube.com/user/rockenroll4life rockenroll4life]。他在那里上传Minecraft Let's Plays。

2019年4月10日，由于瑞典移民局拒绝了他的定居/工作延期请求，他宣布暂时离开Mojang。<ref>{{tweet|rockenroll4life|1115902582101356545|<nowiki>[1/2]</nowiki> Hey Everyone, It comes with heavy heart that I have to announce that due to complications with Swedish Migrations my residency/work extension was denied and I'm no longer able to work in Sweden or at Mojang for the time being. (Unless some miracle happens)|April 10, 2019}}</ref><ref>{{tweet|rockenroll4life|1115902616884666373|<nowiki>[2/2]</nowiki> While I may be flying back to the states soon fear not!

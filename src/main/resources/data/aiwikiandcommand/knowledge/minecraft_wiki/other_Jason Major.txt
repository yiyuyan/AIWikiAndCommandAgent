'''詹森·梅吉尔（Jason Major）'''曾经是Minecraft Redmond工作室的一名成员，同时也是Minecraft[[携带版]]的开发者。<ref name="pe_rabbits">{{tweet|jeb|663657035595186176|Good Monday! @argo_major's rabbit improvements (for MC:PE) have now been added back to MC:PC, and will be included in the next snapshot|November 9, 2015}}</ref>他也写过一本小说。
他也帮助过了许多AAA电子游戏，包括NBA Live 98、Need for Speed: Hot Pursuit和Halo 2等。他在加拿大出生和成长，但现在他和妻子与两个儿子在西雅图居住。

== 你知道吗 ==
* Jason为[[携带版Alpha 0.13.0|Alpha 0.13]]和[[Java版1.9]]中的[[兔子]]做了主要更改。<ref name="pe_rabbits" />
* 在一次Game Jam中，Jason第一个在许多其他生物中提出了[[流浪者]] – [[Jeb]]“认为这些生物很适合我们为[[Java版1.10]]所做的计划”<ref>[https://minecraft.net/en/article/meet-stray They’re based on a concept that emerged from a game jam the Bellevue team had, where they were just adding a bunch of stuff to Minecraft - whatever they wanted. Jason Major made a bunch of cool mobs and I thought these particular ones fitted really well with what we were already planning. So we added them!] – Jens Bergensten, December 16, 2016</ref>
* [[wzh:根汁啤酒|Root beer]]是他最喜欢的饮料之一，他的桌子上经常有一瓶。
* 他偶尔加入官方Minecraft直播，他证实了他会在新的可染色床预览时出现在直播中。

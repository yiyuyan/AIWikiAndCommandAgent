{{about|已经从Java版中移除的配方|基岩版中已移除的配方|基岩版已移除配方}}
{{outdated|edition=java}}
从[[Java版]]开始开发至今，有一些合成配方已经从游戏中移除。这些合成配方可能已经被取代，或者开发人员已决定其今后不会加入游戏。

请注意：该页只记录从游戏中移除的合成配方；移除的合成配方在其对应的历史段落中有描述。

== 合成配方 ==
=== 箭 ===
{{see also|箭}}

[[Java版Indev 0.31 20100128-2304]]加入了用1个[[苹果]]合成箭的测试配方，这个配方在[[Java版Indev 0.31 20100129-1447]]被移除。
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1
|A1=Apple
|Output=Arrow
}}

[[Java版Indev 0.31 20100130]]加入了另一个合成配方，可以通过[[铁锭]]、[[木棍]]、[[羽毛]]合成箭，后于[[Java版Alpha v1.0.14]]被移除。

{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1
|Output=Arrow,4
|B1=Iron Ingot|B2=Stick|B3=Feather}}

=== 铁块、金块和钻石块 ===
{{see also|铁块|金块|钻石块}}

[[Java版Indev 0.31 20100129-1447]]加入了用4个矿物原材料合成矿物块的配方：
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1
  |A2=Iron Ingot; Gold Ingot; Diamond
  |A3=Iron Ingot; Gold Ingot; Diamond
  |B2=Iron Ingot; Gold Ingot; Diamond
  |B3=Iron Ingot; Gold Ingot; Diamond
  |Output=Block of Iron; Block of Gold; Block of Diamond
}}
这些配方在[[Java版Indev 0.31 20100130]]被需要9个矿物原材料的配方取代。

=== 面包 ===
{{see also|面包}}

[[Java版Indev 20100206-2034]]加入了用6个[[小麦]]合成面包的配方：
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1
  |A2=Wheat
  |A3=Wheat
  |B2=Wheat
  |B3=Wheat
  |C2=Wheat
  |C3=Wheat
  |Output=Bread
}}
该配方在[[Java版Indev 20100223]]被需要3个小麦的配方取代。

=== 金蘋果 ===
{{see also|金蘋果}}
金蘋果在加入时需要用8個[[黃金方塊]]和1個[[蘋果]]合成。
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1
|A1=Block of Gold |A2=Block of Gold |A3=Block of Gold
|B1=Block of Gold |B2=Apple         |B3=Block of Gold
|C1=Block of Gold |C2=Block of Gold |C3=Block of Gold
|Output=Golden Apple
}}
[[Java版1.0.0]]改为8個[[金粒]]和1個蘋果合成，最後[[12w23a]]把金粒改成[[金錠]]。
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1
|A1=Gold Nugget |A2=Gold Nugget |A3=Gold Nugget
|B1=Gold Nugget |B2=Apple         |B3=Gold Nugget
|C1=Gold Nugget |C2=Gold Nugget |C3=Gold Nugget
|Output=Golden Apple
}}

=== 锁链盔甲 ===
{{see also|盔甲}}

在[[14w25a]]之前，锁链盔甲可以使用[[火]]合成。在这个快照中，无论是否修改物品栏，火都不再能在物品栏中以物品形式存在，配方本身也从游戏的配方注册表中被移除。
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1
|A1=Fire;Fire;Fire; |B1=Fire;;Fire; |C1=Fire;Fire;Fire;
|A2=Fire            |B2=;Fire;;     |C2=Fire
|A3=;Fire;Fire;Fire |B3=;Fire;;     |C3=;Fire;Fire;Fire
|Output=Chainmail Helmet;Chainmail Chestplate;Chainmail Leggings;Chainmail Boots
}}

=== 附魔金苹果 ===
{{see also|金苹果}}

[[附魔金苹果]]的配方在[[Java版1.3.1]]中被加入，在[[Java版1.9]]的快照[[15w44a]]被移除。
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1
|A1=Block of Gold |A2=Block of Gold |A3=Block of Gold
|B1=Block of Gold |B2=Apple         |B3=Block of Gold
|C1=Block of Gold |C2=Block of Gold |C3=Block of Gold
|Output=Enchanted Golden Apple
}}

=== 马铠 ===
{{see also|马铠}}

在[[Java版1.6.1]]的[[13w16a]]快照中，马铠的配方被加入游戏：
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1
|C1=Iron Ingot; Gold Ingot; Diamond
|A2=Iron Ingot; Gold Ingot; Diamond 
|B2=White Wool
|C2=Iron Ingot; Gold Ingot; Diamond
|A3=Iron Ingot; Gold Ingot; Diamond
|B3=Iron Ingot; Gold Ingot; Diamond |C3=Iron Ingot; Gold Ingot; Diamond
|Output=Iron Horse Armor; Golden Horse Armor; Diamond Horse Armor
}}

该配方于同版本的[[13w18a]]快照中被移除。

=== 堆肥桶 ===
{{see also|堆肥桶}}

在[[Java版1.14]]的[[19w03a]]快照中，堆肥桶的配方被加入游戏：
{{Crafting
|A1= Oak Fence; Spruce Fence; Birch Fence; Jungle Fence; Acacia Fence; Dark Oak Fence|A2= Oak Fence; Spruce Fence; Birch Fence; Jungle Fence; Acacia Fence; Dark Oak Fence
|C1= Oak Fence; Spruce Fence; Birch Fence; Jungle Fence; Acacia Fence; Dark Oak Fence|C2= Oak Fence; Spruce Fence; Birch Fence; Jungle Fence; Acacia Fence; Dark Oak Fence
|A3 = Oak Planks; Spruce Planks; Birch Planks; Jungle Planks; Acacia Planks; Dark Oak Planks|B3 = Oak Planks; Spruce Planks; Birch Planks; Jungle Planks; Acacia Planks; Dark Oak Planks|C3 = Oak Planks; Spruce Planks; Birch Planks; Jungle Planks; Acacia Planks; Dark Oak Planks
|Output = Composter
|type = Miscellaneous
|ignoreusage=1}}

该配方在[[Java版1.15]]的[[19w45a]]快照中被需要7个木台阶的配方取代。

=== 下界合金工具/装备 ===
{{see also|下界合金锭}}

在[[Java版1.16]]的[[20w06a]]快照中，用下界合金锭升级钻石工具及装备的合成配方被加入游戏，后于[[20w10a]]快照被移除：
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1|nocat=1
 |Diamond Axe;Diamond Hoe;Diamond Pickaxe;Diamond Shovel;Diamond Sword;Diamond Helmet;Diamond Chestplate;Diamond Leggings;Diamond Boots
 |Netherite Ingot
 |Output= Netherite Axe;Netherite Hoe;Netherite Pickaxe;Netherite Shovel;Netherite Sword;Netherite Helmet;Netherite Chestplate;Netherite Leggings;Netherite Boots
}}

=== 铜块 ===
{{see also|铜块}}

在[[Java版1.17]]的[[20w46a]]快照中，合成铜块所需的铜锭由9个改为4个，反向分解同理：
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1|nocat=1
 |head=1
 |Copper Ingot
 |Copper Ingot
 |Copper Ingot
 |Copper Ingot
 |Output=Block of Copper
}}
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1|nocat=1
 |Block of Copper, Waxed Block of Copper
 |Output= Copper Ingot,4
 |foot=1
}}
随后的[[21w11a]]加入了将涂蜡的铜块分解为4个铜锭的配方：
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1|nocat=1
 |Waxed Block of Copper
 |Output= Copper Ingot,4
}}
这些配方中的铜锭数量在同版本的快照[[21w17a]]再次被更改为9个。

=== 竹材 ===
{{see also|竹材}}

在[[Java版1.19.3]]的[[22w42a]]快照中，竹材的合成配方被加入游戏，属于[[实验性内容]]“{{experiment|update_1_20}}”：

{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1|nocat=1
 |Bamboo
 |Bamboo
 |Bamboo
 |Bamboo
 |Output=Bamboo Planks}}

该配方在同版本的[[22w45a]]快照中改為由1個[[竹方塊]]分解成2個竹材。

=== 刷子 ===
{{see also|刷子}}

在[[Java版1.19.4]]的[[23w07a]]快照中，刷子的合成配方被加入游戏，属于[[实验性内容]]“{{experiment|update_1_20}}”：
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1|nocat=1
 |A1= String
 |B1= String
 |C1= String
 |B2= Stick
 |B3= Stick
 |Output= Brush
}}

该配方在同版本的预发布版[[Java版1.19.4-pre1]]被1个羽毛、1个铜锭和1个木棍的合成配方取代。

=== 地毯 ===
{{see also|地毯}}

在[[Java版1.14]]的[[18w43a]]快照中，白色地毯的染色配方被加入游戏：
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1|nocat=1
 |A1= White Carpet
 |B1= White Carpet
 |C1= White Carpet
 |A2= White Carpet
 |B2= Matching Colored Dye
 |C2= White Carpet
 |A3= White Carpet
 |B3= White Carpet
 |C3= White Carpet
 |Output= Matching Dyed Carpet,8
}}

该染色配方在[[Java版1.20]]的预发布版[[Java版1.20-pre1]]被单个任意地毯+单个染料的合成配方取代。

=== 铜门 ===
{{see also|铜门}}

在[[Java版1.20.3]]的[[23w43a]]快照中，铜门的合成配方被加入游戏，属于[[实验性内容]]“{{experiment|update_1_21}}”：
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1
|A1= Matching Unwaxed Block of Copper; Matching Waxed Block of Copper |B1= Matching Unwaxed Block of Copper; Matching Waxed Block of Copper
|A2= Matching Unwaxed Block of Copper; Matching Waxed Block of Copper |B2= Matching Unwaxed Block of Copper; Matching Waxed Block of Copper
|A3= Matching Unwaxed Block of Copper; Matching Waxed Block of Copper |B3= Matching Unwaxed Block of Copper; Matching Waxed Block of Copper
|Output= Matching Unwaxed Copper Door,3; Matching Waxed Copper Door,3
}}

在[[Java版1.21]]的[[24w18a]]快照中，合成铜门需要铜锭，而非铜块，铜门的氧化变种不再能够合成。

=== 铜活板门 ===
{{see also|铜活板门}}

在[[Java版1.20.3]]的[[23w43a]]快照中，铜活板门的合成配方被加入游戏，属于[[实验性内容]]“{{experiment|update_1_21}}”：
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1
|A2= Matching Unwaxed Block of Copper; Matching Waxed Block of Copper |B2= Matching Unwaxed Block of Copper; Matching Waxed Block of Copper |C2= Matching Unwaxed Block of Copper; Matching Waxed Block of Copper
|A3= Matching Unwaxed Block of Copper; Matching Waxed Block of Copper |B3= Matching Unwaxed Block of Copper; Matching Waxed Block of Copper |C3= Matching Unwaxed Block of Copper; Matching Waxed Block of Copper
|Output= Matching Unwaxed Copper Trapdoor,2; Matching Waxed Copper Trapdoor,2
}}

在[[Java版1.21]]的[[24w18a]]快照中，合成铜活板门需要铜锭，而非铜块，铜活板门的氧化变种不再能够合成。
{{Crafting|ignoreusage=1
|A2=Copper Ingot |B2=Copper Ingot |C2=Copper Ingot
|A3=Copper Ingot |B3=Copper Ingot |C3=Copper Ingot
|Output=Copper Trapdoor,2}}

在[[Java版1.21.9]]的[[25w32a]]快照中，为与[[铜栏杆]]做区分，更改了铜活板门的合成配方。此前，合成2个铜活板门需要6个铜锭。

'''''Minecraft: Pixel Genesis'''''是由[[Hyper Potions]]、Synthion、AIKA、leon chang、Snail's House、Elliot Hsu和Anamanaguchi创作的混音专辑，于2024年9月13日在多平台发布。<ref>{{ytl|Jj74tRQuC9w|Minecraft Pixel Remixes|Minecraft|September 13, 2024}}</ref>此专辑是''[[Minecraft: Tricky Trials (Original Game Soundtrack)]]''中的曲目与''[[Minecraft: Caves & Cliffs (Original Game Soundtrack)|Infinite Amethyst]]''的混音版本。专辑封面与动画版本由Martin Wörister制作。<ref>{{tweet|adrian_ivl|1834608552717009007|The amazing pixel art is done by @miascugh !|September 13, 2024}}</ref>

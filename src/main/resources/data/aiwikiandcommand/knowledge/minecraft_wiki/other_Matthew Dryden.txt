'''马修·德莱顿（Matthew Dryden）'''在[[Mojang Studios]]担任质量评估专员。他也曾担任过内容协调员与代理内容主管。

== 关于 ==
Moesh在担任内容协调员与代理内容主管时的工作包括协助Minecraft特许内容管理和添加新[[Realms]]地图。他也参与了[[堡垒遗迹]]、[[远古城市]]和[[试炼密室]]等结构的设计。<ref>{{tweet|HeartlessMoesh|1509608453274275841|My work is shown in this week's beta and snapshot for Minecraft! The work I've done was to tune the Ancient City structures so that the Warden had a better time moving around. I also added some evil bits for those who like to build pillars to avoid the Warden...|April 1, 2022|archive-rev=20220331194907}}</ref><ref>{{ytl|2PqhmjPg6FE|Podcast: Structure Design with Moesh|Minecraft Ideas Academy|February 28, 2022}}</ref><ref>{{article|chamber-secrets|The Chamber of Secrets|June 18,2024}}</ref>

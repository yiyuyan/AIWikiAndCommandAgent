'''Super Duper图形包（Super Duper Graphics Pack）'''是一个已经终止开发的{{el|be}}DLC。它于{{w|2017年电子娱乐展}}被宣布<ref>{{Mcnet|all-news-e3|All The News From E3!|June 11, 2017|Tom Stone}}</ref><ref>{{Mcnet|whats-super-duper-graphics-pack|What’s The Super Duper Graphics Pack?|June 15, 2017|Marsh Davies}}</ref><ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170615143116/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1WHYJH-JGk|title=Super Duper Graphics Pack DLC Sneak Peek|website=YouTube|author=TeamMojang|date=June 15, 2017}}</ref>，且在[[MINECON Earth 2017]]得到了进一步宣传<ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171119101442/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Od3lMFZIsE4|title=The Super Duper Minecraft Musical!|website=YouTube|author=TeamMojang|date=November 18, 2017}}</ref>。但由于图像引擎和性能问题<ref name=sdgp>{{citation|url=https://www.windowscentral.com/minecraft-super-duper-graphics-pack|title=Minecraft Super Duper 4K graphics pack: Delay, pricing, and more (update)|website=Windows Central|author=Jez Corden|date=August 12, 2019}}</ref>，Mojang于2019年8月12日宣布放弃开发此项目。<ref>{{Mcnet|super-duper-graphics-pack-ceasing-development|Super Duper Graphics Pack Ceasing Development|August 12, 2019|Staff}}</ref>

Super Duper图形可提供平行光源、动态阴影、高亮边缘、镜面及自发光贴图、摇曳的植被、更多的三维几何、色调映射、视差遮蔽映射、动态曝光、增强的可视距离，以及更细致的全新纹理。原定售价为10.00美元。<ref name=sdgp/>

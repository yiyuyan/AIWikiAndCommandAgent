'''Cobalt披风（Cobalt Cape）'''是向在''[[Cobalt]]''游戏关卡制作大赛与[http://cobaltleague.net/ Cobalt联盟]锦标赛中的参与者/获胜者提供的披风。

== 描述 ==
该披风只提供给关卡制作大赛与[http://cobaltleague.net/ Cobalt联盟]锦标赛中的参与者/获胜者。<ref>{{tweet|jeb_|697463735649234944|As with Scrolls back in the day, the Cobalt tournament has Cobalt capes (for Minecraft) in its prize pool: http://cobaltleague.net/}}</ref>
该披风的鞘翅设计基于''Cobalt''的黄蜂敌人。<ref>{{reddit|6knyx5/my_elytra_texture_looks_like_insect_wings_by|djnrxgr|context=3|It's the wings of the wasp!|jeb_|July 1, 2017}}</ref><ref>{{citation|url=https://mojang.com/2016/02/cobalt-updated-with-new-languages-new-controls-and-a-map-challenge/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160308114916/https://mojang.com/2016/02/cobalt-updated-with-new-languages-new-controls-and-a-map-challenge/|title=Cobalt updated with new languages, new controls and a map-making challenge!|website=Mojang.com|author=Daniel Kaplan|date=February 19, 2016}}</ref><ref name="CWC1 announcement">{{citation|url=https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/357340/view/4581805995805587801|title=The Cobalt Workshop Challenge #1|website=Steam News|date=February 12, 2016}}</ref><ref name="CWC2 announcement">{{citation|url=https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/357340/view/4581805995805586832|title=CWC1 Winners and CWC2 Starting!|website=Steam News|date=March 3, 2016}}</ref><ref name="CWC3 announcement">{{citation|url=https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/357340/view/4581805995805585705|title=Cobalt Workshop Challenge #3|website=Steam News|date=March 24, 2016}}</ref>

Block of Carminite is a block crafted from nine Carminite. This block is mostly decorative, used to store numerous pieces of Carminite into one block. Regardless of what tool was used to break a Block of Carminite, it will always break instantly. Block of Carminite can be crafted back into nine Carminite pieces.

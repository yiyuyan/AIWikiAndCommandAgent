Nether Berry Bushes are the colloquial term for berry bushes that can be found in the Dark Tower. A Nether Berry Bush has 4 growth stages, where the last stage bears fruit. Once a berry bush has been harvested, it will slowly regrow more berries.

In order for a Nether Berry Bush to grow, it will need to be planted on a Nether-native surface such as Netherrack, Soul Sand, or Blackstone. However, if it is planted on Nylium, the berry bush will die.

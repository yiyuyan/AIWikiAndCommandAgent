Naga Scale Armor is armor made from Naga Scales. This armor set consists of only Chestplate and Leggings. Because this armor set is made from the materials from a Boss, the Naga, it comes with quite a high armour value.

__NOTOC__

## Naga Scale Tunic

The Naga Scale Tunic is a part of the Naga Scale Armor set, used in the Chest slot. When crafted, the Naga Scale Tunic comes enchanted with the Fire Protection III enchant. When worn, it has an armor rating of 7, stronger than the Iron Chestplate.

### Crafting

## Naga Scale Leggings

The Naga Scale Leggings is a part of the Naga Scale Armor set, used in the Legs slot. When crafted, Naga Scale Leggings comes enchanted with the Protection III enchant. When worn, it has an armor rating of 6, similar to the Diamond Leggings.

The Firefly is a critter block found commonly throughout the Twilight Forest. They occur naturally on the trunks of Twilight Oak Trees, Mangrove Trees, Canopy Trees, and in Hedge Mazes. A Firefly can be worn in the Helmet slot, though it offers no additional protection and it does not emit light. Fireflies can also be used to craft a Firefly Jar and Firefly Particle Spawner.

When broken, the Firefly will drop itself. However, when shot with an Arrow or crushed with an Anvil, it will drop Glowstone Dust.

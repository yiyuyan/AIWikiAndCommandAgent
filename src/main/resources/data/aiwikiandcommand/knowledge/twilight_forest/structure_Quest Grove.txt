Situated amongst the ferns and rainbow trees in the rare Enchanted Forest biomes, the Quest Grove is an ancient stone structure with weathered and aged arches that serves as the home of the Questing Ram. Explorers have found that these tranquil clearings will always have a Dispenser set into one of the arches - activating it will place a random assortment of coloured Wool blocks. The friendly Ram seems to respond well if these blocks are given to it. Adventurers who have given all 16 colours report that they were rewarded with a bundle filled with the Questing Ram Trophy and several resource blocks - one of Block of Gold, Block of Iron, Block of Copper, Block of Coal, Block of Lapis Lazuli, Block of Emerald, and Block of Diamond, as well as the unique Crumble Horn artifact.

Quest Groves, much like the Enchanted Forest biome itself, does not have any magical protection over it, thus adventurers do not need to find and kill boss creatures such as the Naga before entering these tranquil glades.

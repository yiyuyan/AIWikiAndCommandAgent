The Maze Map is useful mostly for finding your way around the various mazes in the Twilight Forest. Like a vanilla map, it can see past walls and various places that you can normally not see. The Maze Map is special with this, as it will show you a slice of the world at your current depth or altitude, allowing you to find your way through caves and other structures. It is also zoomed in enough to show even the thinnest walls.

__TOC__

## Obtaining
The Maze Map can be only be obtained by crafted with Paper and a Maze Map Focus, locateable only within the Labyrinth.

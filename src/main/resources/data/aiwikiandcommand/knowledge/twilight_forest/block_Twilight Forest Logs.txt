## Obtaining

### Twilight Oak
Twilight Oak Logs are a very common type of log that can be found in the Twilight Forest. As it too is an Oak variant, it shares a similar appearance to the Oak Tree. These logs can be harvested from Twilight Oak Trees, Rainbow Oak Trees and Giant Twilight Oak Trees, but can be seen making up various Landmarks.

### Canopy
Canopy Tree Logs are a common log type, found primarily within the heavier forested parts of the Twilight Forest. These logs are harvested from Canopy Trees, some of which generating as attachments to Hedge Mazes. They also can generate as a "Fallen" variant; lain out onto the ground with occassional patches of moss covering the log.

### Mangrove
Mangrove Logs, not to be confused with their vanilla counterparts, are found within the Twilight Swamp. These logs can also be rarely spotted on Wells, but will primarily generate as a part of the Mangrove Trees along side of Mangrove Roots.

### Darkwood
Darkwood Logs generate within the Dark Forest, and can be found in the Dark Tower as well. Like all other logs, these can generate as part of a Well.

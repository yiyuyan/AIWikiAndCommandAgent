Wild Deer in the Twilight Forest are equivalent to the Cows from the Overworld. Unlike Cows, Wild Deer drop Raw Venison when killed, or Venison Steak when killed while on fire, as well as dropping Leather. They are found commonly in most areas in the Twilight Forest. Wild Deer can be bred with Wheat.

Thorn Roses are plants that grow on Thorns, though do not damage the player depsite the thorny implications. These flowers can be harvested and placed on any kind of wood and on any side of it. Thorn Roses currently cannot be planted in Flower Pot.

## Usage

## Composting
Placing a Thorn Rose into a Composter has a 50% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

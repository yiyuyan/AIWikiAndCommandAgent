Fallen Leaves is a decorative type of foliage typically found in the Spooky Forest. This block behaves similar to Snow, allowing for multiple Fallen Leaves blocks to be placed in a single space to create a bigger pile, and up to 8 layers may be placed. Unlike most Leaves, Fallen Leaves do not have solid collision and can be passed through without obstruction, scattering leaf particles where an entity walked through. A unique property of Fallen Leaves is the ability to be placed on the surface of water, behaving like leaves floating on water. When placed like this, Fallen Leaves behave similarly to Lily Pad, supporting the entity on top and creating a solid surface on top of water.

Fallen Leaves can be harvested with Shears, dropping the amount equal to layers of the block.

## Composting
Placing a Leaf Pile into a Composter has a 10% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

The Scepter of Fortification is a scepter which summons shields around the player or target entity. These shields appear similar to the shields that surround the Twilight Lich during its first phase. However, unlike the Twilight Lich's shields, these ones break on any damage received, with some exceptions to some damage sources such as drowning, falling, or void damage.

On using a Scepter of Fortification, a popping sound is made, and either the player or target entity is surrounded by 5 shields. These shields will break on damage, but will also decay after 12 seconds. These shields can be regenerated again by using the Scepter of Fortification again once the 60 second cooldown clears. These shields generated by the Scepter of Fortification will be the first to break if the user also has shields permanent shields granted by achievements or commands. Broken shields will reset the decay timer.

The Scepter of Fortification holds 9 charges and can be recharged by placing an empty Scepter of Fortification with a Golden Apple or an Exanimate Essence in a crafting table. The Scepter will never break once it's depleted. It can only be enchanted with Renewal.

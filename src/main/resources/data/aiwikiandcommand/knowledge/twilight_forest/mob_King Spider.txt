【生物信息】
类型：Hostile
生命值：30 (♥ x 15)
攻击力：6 (♥♥♥)
掉落：Spider Eye (0-1)
String (0-2)
ID：king_spider

King Spiders can only be found in the Dark Forest and the first sign of them is usually their red eyes glowing in the darkness. They are twice the size, speed and toughness of normal spiders, and are found with a Skeleton Druid riding on their back, making them a formidable foe to deal with.

King Spiders can drop Spider Eyes and String.

Knightmetal Ingot is an item obtained from smelting Armor Shard Clusters. Much like iron, Knightmetal Ingots can be used to craft a variety of useful tools and armor, as well as its own storage block. Knightmetal Ingots can also be used for components of the Block and Chain weapon.

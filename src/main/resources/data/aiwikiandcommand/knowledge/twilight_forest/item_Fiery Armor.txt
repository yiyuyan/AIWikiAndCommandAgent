Fiery Armor is armor made from Fiery Ingots. Before version 2.3.3, crafted Fiery Armor would come automatically enchanted with Fire React, but has since been removed. Each Fiery Armor piece is unable to support the enchantments Thorns, Fire React, and Chill Aura. As this armor set is made from the materials from a Boss, the Hydra and the Ur-Ghast, this armor comes with an armor toughness rating of 1.5.

## Obtaining

### Fiery Helmet

The Fiery Helmet is a part of the Fiery Armor set, worn in the Helmet slot. Fiery Helmet does not come automatically enchanted; however, it has the innate skill of burning attackers, acting similar to Thorns. When worn, it has an armor rating of 4, stronger than the Diamond Helmet.

### Fiery Chestplate

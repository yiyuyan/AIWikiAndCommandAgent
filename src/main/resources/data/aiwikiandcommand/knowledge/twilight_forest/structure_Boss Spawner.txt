The Boss Spawner is a block designed to spawn Bosses. This block is unobtainable in Survival mode, but can be accessed in the Creative inventory. The type of boss spawned from the Boss Spawner depends on the variant obtained.

These blocks generate in structures with bosses, and are set to immediately spawn once the player is in range, unless they are in Peaceful mode, where it will remain idle and generate particles. Changing back to Peaceful when a boss is still alive causes the Boss Spawner to reappear.

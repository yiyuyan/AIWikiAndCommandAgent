Moss Patch is a small patch of moss that comes in different shapes and sizes. They can be found growing on natural features such as Hollow Logs. Moss Patches give off a strange particle similar to Mycelium and can be harvested with Shears.

Placing a Moss Patch into a Composter has a 50% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

Giant Logs look like a much larger log block. It is the size of 64 normal log blocks in a 4x4x4 formation. It can be harvested with any tool, but it takes 64 times as long as a normal log. Unlike the other Giant Blocks, it is impossible to uncraft wooden planks into the Giant Log. Due to the nature of Giant Logs, the tops of the log will burn forever. Giant Log can be used to craft a Giant's Pickaxe, Giant's Sword, or 256 Oak Planks.

Overworld Berries are the colloquial term for berries that can be found scattered throughout Twilight Forest. Despite the name, these berries do not naturally generate in the Overworld.

These berries can provide a quick snack to restore a little hunger. However, combining all four Overworld Berries and a Bowl can create a Berry Medley.

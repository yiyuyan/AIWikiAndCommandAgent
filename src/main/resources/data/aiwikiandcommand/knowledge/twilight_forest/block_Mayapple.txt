Mayapple is a small plant that can be found in patches throughout the Twilight Forest. To survive, Mayapples must be planted on a grass or dirt block near the sunlight. When struck with Shears, they can be harvested for placement in other locations. Mayapple can also be planted in a Flower Pot.

Placing a Mayapple into a Composter has a 30% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

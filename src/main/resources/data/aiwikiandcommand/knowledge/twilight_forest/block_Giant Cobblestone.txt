【方块信息】
hardness：128
tool：Giant Pickaxe

Giant Cobblestone looks like a much larger cobblestone block. It is the size of 64 normal cobblestone blocks in a 4x4x4 formation. It can be harvested with any tool, but it takes 64 times as long as normal cobblestone. The Giant's Pickaxe harvests giant cobblestone at a normal rate. Giant Cobblestone can be used to craft Giant Tools or 64 blocks of Cobblestone.

Scattered amongst the glades and hills are these large lakes, referred to simply as Twilight Lakes by explorers. Although much smaller than the vast oceans found in the Overworld, these can reach down to a surprising depth. Although nothing of note has been found to date in the tranquil waters, there are persistent rumours that islands may eventually be found on the water, with secrets and treasures yet to be discovered. Time will eventually tell, perhaps.

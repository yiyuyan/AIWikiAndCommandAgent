Torchberries are an item found by harvesting Torchberry Plants or slaying Skeleton Druids. They are clusters of 5 small berries that emit light, and can be used in various crafting recipes. Often, Torchberries can be used to craft a Magic Map Focus. Crafting a Torchberry with a Stick, similar to crafting a Torch, will give you five Torch. Combined with the Roots commonly found in Caves that drop Sticks, you are almost guaranteed to have the necessary materials for light underground. You can also use Torchberries to feed a Moonworm Queen, by crafting 3 Torchberries and a Moonworm Queen.

When eaten, Torchberries won't provide any saturation nor hunger bars, but will grant the Glowing for 5 seconds at a 75% rate.

If all other food sources have been eaten, the player will be rewarded with the Advancement: We Dine At Eternal Sundown

## Usage

## Composting
Placing Torchberries into a Composter has a 30% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

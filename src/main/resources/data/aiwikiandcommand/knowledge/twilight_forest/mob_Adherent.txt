Adherent is a monster that used to appear in the Final Castle prior to version 4.2.1428. These monsters look like Wraiths, but black and blue. An Adherent attacks like a Skeleton Druid, shooting Poison projectiles at targets.

Adherents are currently a work-in-progress.

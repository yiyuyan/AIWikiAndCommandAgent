The Carminite Ghastling can be found in the Dark Tower. They are similar to Ghast in appearance, with the exception of being smaller. They mostly leave players alone, but will become agitated when looked directly at. Carminite Ghastlings may drop a Ghast Tear and Gunpowder when slain, similar to the Ghast.

Carminite Ghastlings can also appear after a Carminite Reactor explodes, and the Ur-Ghast can summon several significantly weaker versions of this monster during the boss battle near Ghast Traps.

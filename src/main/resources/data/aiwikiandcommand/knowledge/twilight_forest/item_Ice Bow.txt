Ice Bow is a weapon that can be found in the Aurora Palace. The Ice Bow acts like a normal bow, but when an arrow from an Ice Bow hits a target, they will be covered in ice and inflicted with Frosted III for 10 seconds. This bow is useful to slow down moving targets or make escape from a monster easy. Ice Bows cannot be crafted, but they can be repaired with any type of Ice using an Anvil.

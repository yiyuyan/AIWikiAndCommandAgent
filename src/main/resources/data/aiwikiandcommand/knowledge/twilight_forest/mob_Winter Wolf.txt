Winter wolves are large, bluish-white canines that live in the Snowy Forest. These monsters resembles their  Dark Forest counterparts, but with a white coat. They pursue and attack players in their territory, attacking with both their bite and with a freezing breath attack that applies  Frosted III. They are dangerous at melee range, but they are easily countered by bows.

When slain, Winter Wolves drop up to 2 Arctic Fur.

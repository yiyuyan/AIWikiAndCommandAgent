Raven Feather is an item dropped by Ravens, each one dropping up to 2 of these. Raven Feathers contain a magical property that can be used to craft a Magic Map Focus. One can craft a Magic Map Focus by combining this item, Glowstone Dust and Torchberries in any shape on a crafting bench.

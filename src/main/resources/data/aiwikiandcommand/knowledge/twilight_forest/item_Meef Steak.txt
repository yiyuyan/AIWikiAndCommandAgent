Meef Steak is a food item that can be obtained by cooking Raw Meef, and is dropped whenever a Minotaur dies while it is on fire. When eaten, Meef Steak will provide the player with 6 () points of Hunger and 7.2 points of Saturation.

If all other food sources have been eaten, the player will be rewarded with the Advancement: We Dine At Eternal Sundown

Exanimate Essence is an item that can be found from breaking Sinister Spawners and as loot in the Lich Tower. When used on any solid surface, Exanimate Essence will attempt to light an Ominous Fire. If used on a Candle, it will become an Ominous Candle which appears just like a regular Candle with a purple flame. Using an Exanimate Essence on a Candelabra with normal flame will turn it into ominous lighting, making the flames purple.

Exanimate Essence can be used to recharge the Twilight Scepter, Scepter of Life Draining, Zombie Scepter, and Scepter of Fortification. It can be used as a substitute for the recharging recipes for each Scepter, and can also automatically recharge any Scepter automatically if they are enchanted with Renewal.

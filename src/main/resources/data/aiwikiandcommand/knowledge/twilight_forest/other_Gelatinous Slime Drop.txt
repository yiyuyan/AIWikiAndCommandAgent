Gelatinous Slime Drops are form of food that can be obtained from the Drying Rack. This process requires leaving a slime ball item on the Drying Rack for about 5 minutes.

## Gelatinous Slime Drop

Gelatinous Slime Drop is a food item crafted from Slimeball. It can restore 1 () points of hunger and 0.2 saturation. Once consumed, it will grant 30 seconds of Movement Speed.

## Gelatinous Maze Slime Drop

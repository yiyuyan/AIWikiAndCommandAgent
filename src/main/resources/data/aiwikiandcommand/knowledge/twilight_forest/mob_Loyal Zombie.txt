Loyal Zombie is a special type of Zombie that is summoned by the Zombie Scepter. This variation of the Zombie has a vibrant green shade and is slightly stronger with 1 point more armor and two (♥) more damage than the Zombie on Hard difficulty. The Loyal Zombie attack any target that hurts the summoner, hurts it, or any nearby monsters.

When summoned, the Loyal Zombie will be affected with Strength II for one minute. Once the effect disappears, the Loyal Zombie will start taking 2 (♥ x 1) Expiry damage every second. Feeding a Loyal Zombie Rotten Flesh will reset the timer, affecting the Zombie with another minute of Strength boost and healing the Loyal Zombie by 1 (). Loyal Zombie can also be healed with Potions of Harming as it is classified as an undead mob.

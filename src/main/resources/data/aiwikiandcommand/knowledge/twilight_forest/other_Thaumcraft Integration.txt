Thaumcraft is a mod that adds Thaumaturgy into Minecraft, with additions such as research, Aspects on items, and many more. When Thaumcraft is present with the Twilight Forest Mod, most items gain aspects that can be used for the different parts of Thaumcraft. Below are a list of items and blocks by Aspects, which can be sorted by name or the amount contained for an Aspect.

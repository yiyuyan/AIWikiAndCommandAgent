Carminite Reactors are potentially dangerous Dark Tower devices that can be found in rooms near the top of the Dark Tower. When activated by placing 6 Redstone Block against each face, it will turn green and generate Fake Gold and Fake Diamond Blocks around itself. After a short period of time, it will begin to desintegrate nearby blocks and afterwards explode, spawning 5 Carminite Ghastlings.

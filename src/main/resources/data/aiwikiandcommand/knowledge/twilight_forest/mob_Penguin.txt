【生物信息】
类型：Passive
生命值：10 (♥♥♥♥♥)
攻击力：None
掉落：Feather (0-2)
ID：penguin

Penguins live in great numbers on top of Glaciers and drop Feathers when killed. Penguins are shy creatures, and will attempt to gather themselves into groups, meaning that they may not move from their spot. Unlike Chickens, Penguins can be bred with Raw Fish.

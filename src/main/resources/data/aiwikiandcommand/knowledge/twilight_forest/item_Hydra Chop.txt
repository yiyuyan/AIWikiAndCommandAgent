Hydra Chop is an extremely potent food source, found only from killing a Hydra, each dropping from a range of 5 to 35 of these amazing chops! When consumed they restore hunger and grant the player Regeneration I for 5 seconds. One would be wise enough to consume one of these when their hunger bar is low, as Hydra Chops can restore up to 18 () points of Hunger and 36 points of Saturation.

When one would consume a Hydra Chop while their hunger all the way down to 0, they will be granted the Advancement: Hydra Chop, Baby!

If all other food sources have been eaten, the player will be rewarded with the Advancement: We Dine At Eternal Sundown

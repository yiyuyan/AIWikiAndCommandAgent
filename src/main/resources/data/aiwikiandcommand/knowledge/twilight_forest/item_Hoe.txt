Hoes are tools primarily used to till Dirt and Grass into Farmland. The Twilight Forest mod adds only two types in the form of Ironwood and Steeleaf variants.

## Ironwood Hoe

The Ironwood Hoe is a tool made from Ironwood Ingots. Unlike other Ironwood tools, Ironwood Hoes do not come automatically enchanted. It can be used as a weapon, dealing 1 () damage and has an Attack speed of 3, identical to the Iron Hoe.

### Crafting

## Steeleaf Hoe

The Steeleaf Hoe is a tool made from Steeleaf. When crafted, Steeleaf Hoes have the Fortune II skill automatically enchanted. It can be used as a weapon, dealing 1 () damage, similar to the Diamond Hoe.

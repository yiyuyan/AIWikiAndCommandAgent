The Firefly Forest is a variant of the Twilight Forest biome. While broadly similar to that biome, this part of the forest is distinguished by patches of flowers growing between the trees, along with the curious spectacle of Firefly Jars and Cicada Jars scattered throughout the biome. Most of these jars hang from the trees by a Rope or Chain, while some can be found placed on top of Fence Posts, dotting the forest floor like lamp posts.

Occasionally, Hollow Hills, Hedge Mazes, Naga Courtyards and Lich Towers can be found here, easily spotted as the foliage is not thick in these parts of the forest. Like most forest biomes, Robust Twilight Oak Trees grow in this biome.

No magical protection exists here, allowing adventurers to traverse these tranquil glades without restriction.

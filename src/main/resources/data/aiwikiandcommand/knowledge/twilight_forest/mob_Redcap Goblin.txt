Redcap Goblins are little miners that lurk around in Hollow Hills and sometimes Goblin Knight Strongholds of the Twilight Forest. They carry an Iron Pickaxe, wear Iron Boots, and constantly giggle to themselves. They seem to fear directly approaching their enemies, and will attempt to circle to the back of their targets in order to attack. Be careful, some of them will try to blow up unwary adventurers with TNT.

When slain, Redcap Goblins drop Coal, and they may drop their Iron Boots and Iron Pickaxe, though they may be damaged.

Canopy Bookshelves are decorative blocks that act much like Bookshelf, but only require 1 less book per shelf. This can be very useful for those looking to make a high leveled enchantment set up without as much effort of getting so many books!

__TOC__

## Obtaining
These bookshelves can be made by the player.

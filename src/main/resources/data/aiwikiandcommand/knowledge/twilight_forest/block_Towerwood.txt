The Dark Towers of the Twilight Forest are made up of strong, fire-resistant planks referred to as Towerwood.

__TOC__

## Obtaining

### Towerwood Planks
Towerwood Planks are the primary forms of Towerwood and are obtained from stonecutting unstripped Darkwood Logs.

#### Stonecutting

### Encased Towerwood Planks
Encased Towerwood Planks primarily outline the walls and decorative features on top of the Dark Tower. They are obtained from stonecutting Darkwood Logs or Towerwood Planks.

#### Stonecutting

### Cracked Towerwood Planks
Cracked Towerwood Planks are uniquely obtained from being cooked in a Smoker as opposed to a Furnace.

#### Smoker Smelting

### Mossy Towerwood Planks
Mossy Towerwood Planks are strewn with mossy patches through the cracks and surface, appearing with very faint light-green lines.

#### Crafting

### Infested Towerwood Planks
Infested Towerwood Planks act similar to Infested Block in that they spawn a Towerwood Borer when broken or if a player provokes another Towerwood Borer. There are no means to obtain this block.

## Uses

### Crafting
Towerwood can be used as a replacement for other plank variants to make Pickaxe#Wooden, Chest, Bed, and other wood-based items. They also come with unique crafting recipes as well.

#### Carminite Builder

#### Carminite Reactor

#### Knightmetal Shield

#### Reappearing Block

#### Vanishing Blocks

### Furnace

#### Fuel
Towerwood can also be used to fuel any furnace, acting similar in regards to other plank variants despite their usual fire resistance. All variants burn for 300 ticks or 15 seconds.

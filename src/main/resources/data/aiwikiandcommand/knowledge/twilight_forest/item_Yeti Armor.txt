Yeti Armor is armor made from Alpha Yeti Fur. This armor has the innate property of inflicting the Frosted, the duration and amplification of the effect increasing the more of each piece of armor is worn. Due to this effect, the enchantments Thorns, Fire React, and Chill Aura cannot be applied to Yeti Armor. It also has the ability to ignore the effects of freezing in Powder Snow.

All pieces of Yeti Armor has a toughness value of 3 - the same as Netherite - an enchantment value of 15, and can be repaired in an Anvil with Alpha Yeti Fur.

## Yeti Horned Helm

The Yeti Horned Helm is a part of the Yeti Armor set, used in the Helmet slot. Yeti Horned Helm comes automatically enchanted with Protection II. When worn, it has an armor rating of 4, stronger than the Netherite Helmet.

## Yeti Jacket

The Yeti Jacket is a part of the Yeti Armor set, used in the Chest slot. Yeti Jacket comes automatically enchanted with Protection II. When worn, it has an armor rating of 7, stronger than the Iron Chestplate.

### Crafting

## Yeti Leggings

The Yeti Leggings is a part of the Yeti Armor set, used in the Legs slot. Yeti Leggings comes automatically enchanted with Protection II. When worn, it has an armor rating of 6, similar to the Netherite Leggings.

### Crafting

## Yeti Boots

The Yeti Boots is a part of the Yeti Armor set, used in the Boots slot. Yeti Boots comes automatically enchanted with Protection II and Feather Falling IV. When worn, it has an armor rating of 3, similar to the Netherite Boots.

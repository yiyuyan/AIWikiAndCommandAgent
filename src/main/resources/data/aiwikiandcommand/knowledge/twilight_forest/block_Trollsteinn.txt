hardness：2

Trollsteinn is a purple-speckled rock found in Troll Caves. This block changes appearance depending on the light level, where it will change if the light level is desirable for monster spawns or not. The block will also change in the inventory.

Light purple speckles mean that the surrounding area is bright enough that no hostile mobs will spawn. Dark purple speckles mean that it is dark enough nearby to spawn monsters.

The Rabbit's Pocket Watch is a unique treasure item that grants the player several different effects depending on how its kept on them. It is obtained as rare loot from Medium Hollow Hills.

When placed in a player's hotbar, the Rabbit's Pocket Watch provides both the Speed and Jump Boost effects. It will also grant Haste when the Rabbit's Pocket Watch is held in the main or off-hand. These effects do not stack and no additional effects are given for holding multiple Rabbit's Pocket Watches.

In addition to providing beneficial effects, the Rabbit's Pocket Watch will prevent the holder from receiving the Mining Fatigue effect as long as it's held in the player's main hand or off-hand. The Rabbit's Pocket Watch will not clear any pre-existing Mining Fatigue affecting the player, and will only prevent new instances of Mining Fatigue from being applied.

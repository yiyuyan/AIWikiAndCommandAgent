Traveller's Boots are a piece of Traveller's Gear that can be worn in the Boots slot. Unlike most equipment, Traveller's Boots will not break when durability is completely reduced. Instead, the ability and modifiers are rendered useless and it will no longer have any armor value.

When equipped, the Traveller's Boots comes with the Step-Up ability, which allows the wearer to increase their step height. This means the wearer can walk up a single block of space instead of jumping.

## Crafting

## Modifiers
There are 5 additional modifiers that can be crafted into Traveller's Boots.

### Auto-Repair
This modifier allows for slow self-repair. The repair process does not require any materials and happens over time. In addition, repairing is sped up in direct sunlight, allowing for the gear to last longer. However, there is a 10 second cooldown for any possibility of self-repair after the armor piece has taken any durability damage which can lead to rapid decline in combat.

### Water Walk
This modifier adds the ability to walk on water. While it allows for walking on water, it does not allow for sprinting. Falling onto water will still cancel out fall damage. Water Walk can be cancelled by holding Shift, causing the wearer to sink into the water.

### Slimy Soles
This modifier negates damage caused by falling. In addition, the wearer will bounce back up as if they are bouncing on a Block of Slime. This bouncing effect reduces in height with each bounce until the wearer comes to a standstill.

### Straight Ahead
This modifier lets the wearer move faster, but only in the forward direction. Movement to the left or right will behave as standard movement, only forward movement is affected.

### Unrestrained
This modifier prevents blocks and block surfaces from slowing you down. Blocks such as Soul Sand, Slime, Honey, Sweet Berry Bushes, etc. will no longer slow down your movement, and slippery movement caused by Ice and its variants is negated. In addition, Unrestrained allows the wearer to sprint on water if Water Walk is present.

Trollvidr (also known as Troll Vines) are grayish-green vines growing in the Troll Caves. They are frequently covered in Trollber. Trollvidr resembles Root Strands, growing from the ceilings of caves, except Trollvidr can only be placed on Stone.

## Composting
Placing Trollvidr into a Composter has a 50% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

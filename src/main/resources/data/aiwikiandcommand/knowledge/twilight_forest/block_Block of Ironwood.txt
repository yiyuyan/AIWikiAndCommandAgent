Block of Ironwood is a block crafted from nine Ironwood Ingots. This block is mostly decorative, used to store numerous pieces of Ironwood Ingots into one block and can be used as a beacon base. Blocks of Ironwood can be crafted back into nine Ironwood Ingots.

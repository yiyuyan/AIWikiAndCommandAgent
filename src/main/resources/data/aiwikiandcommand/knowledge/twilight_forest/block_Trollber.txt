Trollber, or troll berries, are eggplant-colored berries growing in clusters on Trollvidr, inside the Troll Caves. When they are exposed to light, they will gradually ripen. Sometimes slaying a Troll near an unripe cluster will ripen all of them. Ripe clusters of Trollber glow brightly, lighting up the surrounding area.

If there are enough Trollber growing close together, a single cluster ripening will cause a chain reaction, lighting up large sections of the Troll Caves.

Right clicking Trollber will drop Torchberries, and will convert the block into Trollvidr. Trollber cannot be regrown with Bonemeal.

## Composting
Placing Trollber into a Composter has a 65% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

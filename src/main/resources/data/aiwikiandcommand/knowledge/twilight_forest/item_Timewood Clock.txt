Timewood Clock is a core that grows in a Tree of Time. This core looks like a Timewood Log, except for a gray Clock-like shape in the middle. When right-clicked, the core will change to display a fully-colored Clock, as well as emitting a periodic ticking noise.

When in the active state, certain blocks may begin to react. For crop-like blocks, the growth chance is doubled, increasing the chances of a mature crop. Other blocks with a ticking Block Entity - such as a Furnace - will have the tick rate doubled, speeding up the block's processing time. This affects any block within a 16-block radius from the core.

Timewood Clock cannot be harvested and will only drop a Timewood Log when broken. As such, caution must be taken to prevent the Timewood Clock from breaking.

Maniacal Dendrologist is an Advancement added to The Twilight forest in Version 3.9.984 which consists of obtaining all natural-like blocks and their leading recipes.

## Tree Requirements
To complete the advancement you will need these tree materials for all Twilight Forest Trees and Magic Trees:

* Sapling
* Leaves
* Log
* Stripped Log
* Hollow Log
* Wood
* Stripped Wood
* Planks
* Stairs
* Slabs
* Fence
* Fence Gate
* Banister
* Pressure Plate
* Button
* Door
* Trapdoor
* Sign
* Hanging Sign
* Boat
* Chest Boat
* Chest
* Trapped Chest
* Drying Rack

## Extra Items
The last extra bits you will need are:
* Giant Log
* Giant Leaves
* Liveroots (Block)
* Liveroot (Item)
* Mangrove Roots
* Roots
* Root Strands
* Hedge
* Fallen Leaves
* Thorn Leaves
* Thorn Rose
* Huge Stalk
* Beanstalk Leaves
*  All 4 obtainable Towerwood Variants
* Rainbow Oak Leaves
* Robust Twilight Oak Sapling
* Rainbow Oak Sapling
* Canopy Bookshelf
* Chiselled Canopy Bookshelf OR Empty Canopy Bookshelf

You will NOT need any foliage like Mayapples or Moss Patches, nor will you need any Critters like Fireflies or Cicadas.

Heart of Transformation is a core that grows in a Tree of Transformation. This core looks like a Transwood Log, except for a gray heart in the middle. When right-clicked, the core will change to display a light blue heart, as well as emitting a harp Note Block sound.

When in the active state, the biome the Heart of Transformation in will progressively shift into an Enchanted Forest biome, bringing along with it an altered climate and mob spawn pool. Once a biome has been altered to an Enchanted Forest, the transformation is permanent, unable to revert even if the Heart of Transformation is turned off. This affects the region within a 16-block radius from the core.

Heart of Transformation cannot be harvested and will only drop a Transwood Log when broken. As such, caution must be taken to prevent the Heart of Transformation from breaking.

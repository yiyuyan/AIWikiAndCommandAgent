Decorating the Final Castle are special blocks called Castle Brick Tiles. Like all other Castle Bricks, if these blocks are broken, a tool's durability is severely reduced by 17 unless it is the Mazebreaker. There are two variants of Castle Brick Tiles, both of which can be crafted or stonecut. Each tile looks like the top of their respective pillar variant.

## Bold Castle Brick Tile
【方块信息】
hardness：100
tool：Diamond Pickaxe

Bold Castle Brick Tiles is a type of Castle Pillar that is crafted from Bold Castle Brick Pillar. These blocks have a 2x2 checker board pattern on all sides. These blocks can be used to craft Bold Castle Brick Stairs.

### Crafting

## Encased Castle Brick Tile
【方块信息】
hardness：100
tool：Diamond Pickaxe

Encased Castle Brick Tiles are a type of Castle Brick Tile that is crafted from Encased Castle Brick Pillars. These blocks have a black emboss in the shape of a cross, similar to the top and bottom of the Encased Castle Brick Pillar. These blocks can be used to craft Encased Castle Brick Stairs.

Fiddlehead is a decorative block that can be naturally found in the Enchanted Forest. When struck with Shears, they can be harvested for placement in other locations. They do not require tilled soil, and can be placed on any dirt block, similar to Grass. Fiddlehead can also be planted in a Flower Pot.

Placing a Fiddlehead into a Composter has a 30% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

Hedge Mazes are minor landmark found scattered throughout Twilight Forest. These structures can be found dotted throughout common biomes, generating in a grid-like pattern that can be seen from a Magic Map.

The walls of the Hedge Maze are primarily made of Hedge blocks, with dense, prickly foliage to deter adventurers from simply chopping their way through the twisting corridors. Fireflies and Jack-o-Lanterns decorate the Hedge Maze to light up the surrounding area. The entrances to the Hedge Maze are decorated with Canopy Trees.

Hedge Mazes are not a part of progression and can be visited at any time.

## Monsters
In various open spaces, there are spawners that will spawn enemies such as Hostile Wolves, Hedge Spiders, and Swarm Spiders. These monsters can spawn in all light levels and cannot have spawns disabled by lighting up the area around the spawner.

## Loot
Food and adventuring supplies such as Ladder and Arrow are the main rewards from hedge maze chests. Assorted loot such as Diamond Hoe, Shears, and Saddle can be found occasionally.

Rarely, a Music Disc containing the track "Motion" can be found.

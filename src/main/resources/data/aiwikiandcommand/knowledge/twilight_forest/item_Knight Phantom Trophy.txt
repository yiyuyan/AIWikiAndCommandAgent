The Knight Phantom Trophy is a trophy rewarded after slaying the Knight Phantom. Unlike most trophies, and alongside the Minoshroom Trophy, these have an Iron-plated back, whereas the others are Gold-plated. Although this trophy is usable on a Trophy Pedestal, it cannot be used on the pedestal if following the Progression system until at least one Goblin Knight Stronghold is conquered and the Knight Phantom Trophy is taken from the loot chest.

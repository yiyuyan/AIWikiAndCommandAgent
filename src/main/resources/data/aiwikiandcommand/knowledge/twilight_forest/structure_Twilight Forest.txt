The archetypical Twilight Forest is likely the first thing a new explorer sees upon arrival into the Twilight Forest dimension. Canopy Trees and Sickly Twilight Oak Trees grow thickly throughout the lands, and occasional Robust Twilight Oak Trees grow tall above the ground, shrouding the land beneath them. On the ground, various woodland creatures such as Squirrels and Dwarf Rabbits can be found as in other biomes as well as ferns, tall grass, and the occasional patch of mushrooms. Within this luscious flora and fauna, Hedge Mazes, Hollow Hills, Naga Courtyards, and Lich Towers can and have been found throughout the forest.

These areas can be freely explored without putting oneself in unnecessary danger.

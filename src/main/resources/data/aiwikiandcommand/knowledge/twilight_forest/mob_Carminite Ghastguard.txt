Carminite Ghastguards are similar to the regular Nether-exclusive Ghasts other than the fact that they spawn outside Dark Towers. These Carminite Ghastguards are bigger than Carminite Ghastlings and do a similar stalking motion. Carminite Ghastguards may drop a Ghast Tear and Gunpowder when killed.

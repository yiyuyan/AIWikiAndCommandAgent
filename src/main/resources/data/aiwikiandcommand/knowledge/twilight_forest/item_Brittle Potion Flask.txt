Brittle Potion Flask is an item that can store multiple doses of a single Potion. It can be found as rare loot in the Lich Tower.

The Brittle Potion Flask can be filled by right-clicking the item with a Potion. Once filled, the Brittle Potion Flask can no longer be stacked and can only be filled with more doses of the same Potion. Upon consuming a Potion from the Brittle Potion Flask, it will begin to crack, preventing it from being stacked even when empty and reducing the Potion storage capacity by one dose. Once a Brittle Potion Flask has been used 3 times it will break.

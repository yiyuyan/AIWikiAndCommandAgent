Thorns are large, spiky plants that grow all over the Thornlands and can sprawl into neighbouring biomes like the Highlands. Similar to Cactus, touching Thorns will deal the player 4 (♥♥) of damage.

Thorns cannot be burnt with regular fire, and breaking a Thorn block will only sprout more Green Thorns. The only way to remove Thorns is to burn them with a Lamp of Cinders, then tap or walk into the Burnt Thorns.

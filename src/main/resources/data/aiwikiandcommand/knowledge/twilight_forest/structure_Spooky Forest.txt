The Spooky Forest is an unsettling sight to behold to travellers throughout the Twilight Forest dimension. Canopy Trees which once bore a large count of leaves no longer do here. Instead, these Fallen Leaves are found littered on the ground in piles and ocassionally on the water. The landscape is dominated by tall and forlorn trees shorn of all foliage, along with normal sized oak and birch trees here and there. Cobwebs hang from the trees, while Jack o'Lantern can be found standing on fence posts, standing as creepy lamp posts in this biome.

The normally vibrant colours of the forest are instead an autumn like orange colour. Uniquely, even the sky itself has been observed to change from the normal blue/green Twilight sky to a deep, unnatural purple colour, adding to the general unease adventurers have felt when exploring these areas. Also unique to this biome are the Graveyards, cobweb infested places of rest that may hold hidden treasures, but also hidden dangers. Hollow Hills, Naga Courtyards, and Lich Towers have also been found here.

Although all these factors suggest some dark force at work, explorers have found no obstacle to entering these benighted parts of the Forest, thus free exploration is always available here.

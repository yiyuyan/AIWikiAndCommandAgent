Force Fields are blocks that generates in the Final Castle. These blocks appears similar to a Glass Pane, but with particular patterns and can be placed horizontally as well as vertically. It is also possible to create "half" Force Field variants by crouching while placing these blocks. This type of block cannot be broken and is immune to explosions, and thus is only accessable via the creative menu and commands. The colour of these Force Fields are purely aesthetic, though many of these Force Fields can be found in various regions of the Final Castle.

* Violet Force Fields can be located at the top of the castle, acting as a replacement to the wooden fence post cage.
* Pink Force Fields can be located at the bottom of the castle, as a gateway surrounding a staircase into the Castle's basement. It can also be found in the Castle's basement.
* Blue Force Fields can be located at a specific, large tower that is connected to the castle. It too can be found within the Castle's basement.
* Green Force Fields can only be located within the Castle's basement.
* Orange Force Fields cannot be located anywhere as they do not generate anywhere as of now.

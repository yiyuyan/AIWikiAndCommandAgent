The Alpha Yeti Trophy is one of the rewards granted after conquering the Alpha Yeti. Similarly to the Minoshroom Trophy and Knight Phantom Trophy, this trophy has the Alpha Yeti's head set on a silver plate. As all other Trophies within the Twilight Forest, it can be worn in the Helmet slot, though it provides no benefit aside from being cosmetic. It can be used on the Trophy Pedestal in the Goblin Knight Stronghold to open the dungeon. It can also be right-clicked to have it produce Alpha Yeti sounds along with tear particles from the boss when it is throwing a tantrum.

Music Discs are a common staple of Minecraft, providing unique soundtracks to listen to via a Jukebox. Within the Twilight Forest, there are nine unique discs that can be found throughout the dimension, hidden across a variety of lesser found locations. These tracks are created by Rotch Gwylt and MrCompost, and can occassionally be heard as ambience when exploring throughout the dimension.

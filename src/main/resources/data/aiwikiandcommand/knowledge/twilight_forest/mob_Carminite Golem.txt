Guarding the Dark Towers are Carminite Golems. These monsters are a hostile version of the Iron Golem with visible differences. When they see adventurers, they will walk up to them and start throw-attacking them, similar to Iron Golems. Killing a Carminite Golem yields Iron Ingots and Towerwood Planks.

Huge Water Lily is a plant that grows in the Swamp biome. These blocks can be placed on water like Lily Pads, except these are large flowers the size of a block.

## Usage

## Composting
Placing a Huge Water Lily into a Composter has a 65% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

Swarm Spiders are small, purple spiders that appear in great numbers in Hollow Hills, Leaf Dungeons, and Hedge Mazes. Swarm Spiders are weak to the point that most of the time it will harmlessly touch you rather than deal damage, although it still has a low chance to actually hit you.

When slain, Swarm Spiders drop String and a Spider Eye, much like a regular Spider.

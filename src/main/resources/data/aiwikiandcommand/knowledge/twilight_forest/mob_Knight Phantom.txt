The Knight Phantom is a boss that appears in the Goblin Knight Stronghold. They appear as ghostly pieces of Phantom Armor, and appear to have a spooky, ghost-like look when attacking. These Knight Phantoms float in either a circle or a wave, automatically grouping into a single formation if a Knight Phantom detects other nearby Knight Phantom.

## Combat
The Knight Phantoms have two appearances. The first is their intangible appearance, only showing as a set of Phantom Armor and equipment of choice. In this appearance, the Knight Phantom will be harder to strike. The only damage a Knight Phantom can deal in this state is from colliding with the boss.

Their second appearance is more tangible, revealing an apparition wearing Phantom Armor. In this form, they are easier to strike and more damage is dealt in this state. Also in this state, the Knight Phantom will make use of their equipment. Depending on what weapon they are using will change the attack they will perform:
* Knightmetal Sword - Charges towards targets.
* Knightmetal Axe - Throws Knightmetal Axes in the direction of the target.
* Knightmetal Pickaxe - Throws Knightmetal Pickaxes in 5 directions, not aimed at any target.

On Hard difficulty, one Phantom Knight will be given a Knightmetal Shield in the off-hand.


## Treasure
Once all Knight Phantoms are defeated, a treasure chest will drop the Knight Phantoms' gear, such as Knightmetal Weapons, Phantom Armor, and a Knight Phantom Trophy.

Sometimes, the Knight Phantoms will drop their own equipment. These are separate from the equipment found in the chest after defeating all Knight Phantoms.

## Progression
Using the Progression system, the area beneath the trees in the Dark Forest will be filled with smoky ribbons of dark magic. You will be affected with blindness if you stay in the area. Defeating the Twilight Lich will allow you to enter unimpeded. You will also need any boss trophy to unlock the lower levels of the Goblin Knight Stronghold.

Defeating the Knight Phantoms will progress the Dark Forest progression branch, removing the protections over the Dark Forest Center and the Dark Tower.

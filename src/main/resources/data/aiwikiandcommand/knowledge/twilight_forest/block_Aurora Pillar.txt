Aurora Pillar is a decorative block crafted from Aurora Blocks. It can be found decorating the Aurora Palace in certain rooms. Unlike the block counterpart, the colours of an Aurora Pillar range from teal to cyan and do not pulse. Aurora Pillars can be placed at different directions, based on where the player was looking when placing the blocks.

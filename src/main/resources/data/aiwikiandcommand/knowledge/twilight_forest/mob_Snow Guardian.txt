Snow Guardians are monsters endemic to the Aurora Palace. They appear as floating suits of armor, filled only by a stream of snowflakes. Snow Guardians may spawn with Ironwood, Steeleaf, Knightly and Arctic Armor, along with wielding weapons of the same material (except for Arctic, which use the Knightmetal Sword).

When slain, Snow Guardians drop Snowball, and they may drop their equipped tools and armor, although damaged.

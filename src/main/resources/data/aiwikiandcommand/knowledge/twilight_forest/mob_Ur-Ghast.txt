The Ur-Ghast can be found flying around at the top of the gigantic Dark Tower. It appears as a large, supernatural creature with many large, waving tentacles. It is covered in what adventurers fear is blood, or could be Carminite. This creature resembles the Carminite Ghastguard, due to their ability to stalk players, as well as the Nether-exclusive Ghast, but is much larger and much more dangerous.

## Combat
The Ur-Ghast is fought atop the Dark Tower, floating between the four rooms and central room. Due to this, combat will require utilising ranged weaponry or with Ghast Traps that will be located around the arena.

### Fireballs
The Ur-Ghast's main and signature attack. Like all Ghasts, it can shoot fireballs at the challenger, but it will shoot three of these at once. Deflecting these fireballs is possible, however it is dangerous to do so because only one fireball is likely to be deflected while the rest of them will explode around the adventurer. The Ur-Ghast will only shoot fireballs if the adventurer is in view, so taking shelter frequently is advisable.

### Summon Minions
The Ur-Ghast has a special ability to summon Carminite Ghastlings with a lightning strike. These minions will spawn around Ghast Traps. They are weaker than regular Ghastlings as they can be struck with a single hit. Adventurers are advised to be very cautious when they hear the lightning strike of an Ur-Ghast.

### Tantrum Mode
When the Ur-Ghast reaches half the total health, it will begin a Tantrum Mode. It will change the weather to rain and keep on summoning minions. During this mode, the Ur-Ghast will not attack the player and only make loud wails and cry Ghast tears. If the player is under the Ur-Ghast while it's crying, the player will get hurt if hit by a tear. Because the Ur-Ghast is not shooting fireballs when it spots the player, it is the perfect opportunity to attack.

Note that this mode occurs when it is below half and above quarter of the total health remaining. Once it goes below this threshold, it will leave Tantrum Mode and resume attacking with Fireballs.

## Treasure
Once an adventurer slays a Ur-Ghast, a treasure chest will appear in the middle of the battle area. This chest will contain several doses of the Ur-Ghast's  Fiery Tears as well as a few pieces of Carminite. Inside the chest will also contain the Ur-Ghast Trophy. The Ur-Ghast will also drop many levels of experience.

The Ur-Ghast itself also drops Gunpowder and a Ghast Tear, but there is a chance that the adventurer will never get these items, as they may fall off the tower, and by the time the player finds these items, they would have despawned.

## Progression
Using the Progression system, you will need to have defeated the Twilight Lich before entering the Dark Forest. Additionally, the Dark Tower, and all of its blocks and monsters, will be protected until the player has defeated the Knight Phantoms.

Defeating the Ur-Ghast will fully complete the Dark Forest Phase of Progression. If the Ur-Ghast was the last of the three bosses to defeat, the Highlands shall be unlocked.

Currently, nine Custom Banner Patterns are available within Twilight, of which one is partially implemented.

= Obtaining =
Similar to their Banner Pattern, which exist as structure loot or may be crafted with Paper and the corresponding item, Twilight Banner Patterns are created by combining Paper with Trophies obtained from various defeated Bosses:

Note: Questing Ram Swirls Pattern is currently unobtainable, even though the Questing Ram Trophy exists. Additionally, the actual textures for this pattern appear blank.

Inserting these different Banner Patterns within a Loom alongside any Banner and Dye produces the expected print, and these decorated banners can be combined a Shield to apply the respect patterns.

Terrorcotta is a set of decorative building blocks which function not unlike Glazed Terracotta. On placement, these blocks rotate to match the direction in which they were placed or where the player is looking which allows for the assembly of complex patterns.

* Terrorcotta Lines feature diagonal lines and place depending on the direction the player faces.
* Terrorcotta Curves feature a curved pattern and like Lines place depending on the direction the player faces.
* Terrorcotta Arcs feature lines on its sides and a circular pattern on its ends. It places depending on the block face you're clicking on like logs or pillars.

Sometimes while exploring the Leaf Dungeons, an adventurer may spot a strange-looking sapling. These saplings are rare, but magical, and each one will grow into a unique  Magic Tree.

## Tree of Time Sapling
【方块信息】
hardness：0
tool：Any Tool
transparent：Yes

The Tree of Time Sapling is a sapling that grows into a Tree of Time. This sapling can be found rarely in Leaf Dungeons.

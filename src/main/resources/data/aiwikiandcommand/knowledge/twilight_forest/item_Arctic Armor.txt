Arctic Armor is armor made from Arctic Fur. This armor set is quick and easy to craft once the adventurer has unlocked the Snowy Forest, requiring only Yetis or Winter Wolves to harvest materials from. However, Arctic Armor has the same armor rating as Ironwood Armor and only half the durability. Like Leather Armor, Arctic Armor can be dyed into any color.

__NOTOC__

## Arctic Hood

The Arctic Hood is a part of the Arctic Armor set, used in the Helmet slot. Arctic Hood does not come with enchantments when crafted. When worn, it has an armor rating of 2, similar to the Iron Helmet.

## Arctic Jacket

The Arctic Jacket is a part of the Arctic Armor set, used in the Chest slot. Arctic Jacket does not come with enchantments or skills when crafted. When worn, it has an armor rating of 7,  stronger than the Iron Chestplate.

## Arctic Leggings

The Arctic Leggings is a part of the Arctic Armor set, used in the Legs slot. Arctic Leggings does not come with enchantments or skills when crafted. When worn, it has an armor rating of 5, similar to the Iron Leggings.

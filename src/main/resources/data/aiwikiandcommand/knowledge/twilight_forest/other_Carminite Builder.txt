Carminite Builder blocks are a unique and useful Dark Tower Device that can be found in some rooms in the Dark Tower. Once turned on with a Lever, they will begin to build a bridge wherever you are facing. The direction of the bridge will depend on which way the player is facing, and will slowly disappear behind the player. These blocks are very useful when trying to get to another platform separated by a large gap. Carminite Builder Blocks can be broken, harvested and crafted.

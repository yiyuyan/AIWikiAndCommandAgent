Uberous Soil is a dark, loamy dirt block found in patches on the Highlands. It is infused with growth potential, and most plants or crops planted on it will immediately grow fully. After causing this effect, the Uberous Soil block will transform into a block capable of sustaining the plant, such as Dirt, Grass Block, or Farmland, and on rare occasions it can even create Mycelium!

Uberous Soil cannot be collected naturally, but can be found in the Troll Caves. Breaking it with any tool gives only Dirt instead, even with Silk Touch. However, it reacts to Bone Meal, so it can be spread to adjancent Dirt or Grass Blocks, similar to Nylium.

Adventures have found that Magic Beans can be planted in Uberous Soil, causing something very magical to happen.

## Composting
Placing Uberous Soil into a Composter has a 65% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

Hedge Spiders are almost identical to normal overworld Spiders aside from their distinct green color. The only difference between this species and their Overworld counterparts is that they are able to spawn in brightly-lit Hedge Mazes. Legend says these Spiders are brave enough to protect their treasure from explorers.

Similar to overworld Spiders, Hedge Spiders drop Spider Eyes and String when killed.

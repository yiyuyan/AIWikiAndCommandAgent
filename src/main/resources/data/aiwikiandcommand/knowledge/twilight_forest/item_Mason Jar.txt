The Mason Jar is a decorative block for storing an item for display. An item can be inserted by right-clicking the Mason Jar with the item in hand. Items can also be taken out of a Mason Jar by right-clicking with an empty hand. If the Mason Jar is empty or the player is sneaking while right-clicking, it will do a small wiggle, as well as displaying the contents of the Mason Jar if Shift was held down. A Mason Jar can also be used to craft a Firefly Jar, Firefly Particle Spawner or Cicada Jar.

The lid of the Mason Jar can also be changed by right-clicking the Mason Jar with another Log, Stripped Log, Wood, or Stripped Wood block. Only during the week of Halloween, a Pumpkin can also be used as a Mason Jar lid.

When broken, Mason Jars will keep the contents of the jar.

Perhaps related to Unstable Ice Cores, Stable Ice Cores are monsters that appear as a floating head surrounded by a spinning crest of ice crystals. They attack at range, projecting magical snowballs at intruders in the palace. These monsters can be proven annoying, especially when scaling the Aurora Palace and you are not on stable ground. if surrounded by a horde of these, one must escape for their dear life due to their frequent attacking, annoying knockback, and hefty damage output.

When slain, Stable Ice Cores drop Snowball.

Harbinger Cube is a monster that used to appear in the Final Castle prior to version 4.2.1428. These monsters appear like large black and red cubes with small feet/hooves.

Harbinger Cube is work-in-progress and doesn't seem to have the ability to interact with the player despite having damage values for attacking.

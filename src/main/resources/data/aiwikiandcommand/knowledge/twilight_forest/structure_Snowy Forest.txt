The Snowy Forest is an unnaturally cold biome that stands in stark contrast to much of the rest of the Twilight Forest dimension. Explorers have noted many similarities to the Overworld Snowy Taiga biome, with snow covering the ground between large stands of spruce trees growing in place of the usual Canopy Trees, with some growing just as large. Why such an icy climate should exist in a predominantly mild and wet dimension is a mystery, though some suspect the large Glacier that nestles amongst the fir trees may have something to do with it.

Care must be taken when trekking through these areas, as Winter Wolves roam the land along with dangerous Yetis. These creatures should be approached cautiously, especially when delving into the Yeti Caves, as many dozens can be found in these icy caves. The Alpha Yeti who claim dominion over their kin will not take kindly to intrusion from the curious, or the foolish.

## Progression
The chilly climate would be bad enough, but there also seems to be a perpetual howling blizzard that sweeps through these biomes. Attempts to penetrate it have all failed, with explorers finding themselves slowing in their movement regardless of any protective gear. The only way to lift the enchantment is to find and slay a Twilight Lich in his Lich Tower, rendering this area if not comfortable, then at least traversable.

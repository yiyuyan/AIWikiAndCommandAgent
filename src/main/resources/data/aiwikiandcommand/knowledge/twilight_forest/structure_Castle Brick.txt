Building up the majority of the Final Castle, Castle Bricks are decorative blocks mimicing the appearance of Stone Bricks, but with a white base tone and purple tints throughout it's bricks. To prevent anyone from easily breaking their way through the castle, these sturdy bricks will reduce a tool's durability by 17, unless it is the Mazebreaker which is used.

## Castle Brick
Castle Bricks are by far the most commonly found variant of this block family. It would take quite the blind eye for one to be unable to find one of these bricks inside of the Final Castle.

## Worn Castle Brick
Worn Castle Bricks are very faded in appearance compared to the other Castle Brick variants, giving the illusion of being eroded over time from the Acid Rain.

## Mossy Castle Brick
Mossy Castle Bricks are strewn with patches of teal moss. These blocks do not generate naturally in the Final Castle.

### Crafting

## Thick Castle Brick
Thick Castle Bricks, unlike most Castle Brick variants has no gaps or cracks and only a smooth surface. These can most commonly be found in the basements of the Final Castle.

### Crafting

## Bold Castle Brick Pillar
Bold Castle Brick Pillars are a type of Bold Castle Brick which is crafted from 4 Thick Castle Bricks, or are alternatively stonecut from any regular Castle Brick variant. These blocks have a slightly darker emboss though the middle of the pillar on the sides, while the tops and bottoms have a lighter square in the cross-pattern. These blocks generate inside of the Final Castle, standing as large pillars and generate along the bridges, functioning as impractical fences.

### Crafting

## Encased Castle Brick Pillar
Encased Castle Brick Pillars are a type of Encased Castle Brick which is crafted from a mixture of Castle Brick variants as well as Bold or Encased Castle Bricks, or are alternatively stonecut from Castle Bricks or Encased Castle Brick Tiles. These blocks have a black emboss though the middle of the pillar on their sides, while the tops and bottoms have a cross-pattern. Encased Castle Brick Pillars do not generate within the Final Castle, although they did in the past.

### Crafting

## Bold Castle Brick Tile
Bold Castle Brick Tiles are a type of Bold Castle Brick which is crafted from 4 Bold Castle Brick Pillars, or are alternatively stonecut from any regular Castle Brick variant. These blocks have a 2x2 checker board pattern on all sides. Bold Castle Brick Tiles do not generate within the Final Castle.

### Crafting

## Encased Castle Brick Tile
Encased Castle Brick Tiles are a type of Encased Castle Brick which can be crafted from 4 Encased Castle Brick Pillars, or are alternatively stonecut from Castle Bricks. These blocks have a black emboss in the shape of a cross, similar to the top and bottom of the Encased Castle Brick Pillar. Encased Castle Brick Tiles do not generate within the Final Castle.

### Crafting

## Castle Roof Tile
Castle Roof Tiles are a special variant of Castle Brick which can be stonecut from Castle Bricks. Unlike most variants of Castle Brick, this block is completely dark and mimics the look of Mazestone. This block generate on the roof of the Final Castle Towers.

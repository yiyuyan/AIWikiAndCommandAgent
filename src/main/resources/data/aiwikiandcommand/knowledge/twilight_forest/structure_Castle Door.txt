Castle Doors are blocks that generates in the Final Castle. This block is similar to the Reappearing Block found within the Dark Tower in functionality. When right-clicked, the block and any adjacent blocks will vanish in a haze of black particles. These blocks cannot be destroyed by explosions but can be harvested with the right tool.

Castle Doors come in four different colors. The colors are purely aesthetic, but they seem to generate in specific doorways throughout the castle.

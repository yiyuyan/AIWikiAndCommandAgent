Locked Vanishing Blocks are a special kind of Dark Tower Device that appears in the Dark Tower. These act just like regular Vanishing Blocks except you need a Tower Key to unlock them. Once unlocked and right-clicked, they will vanish permanently. These blocks cannot be broken, harvested, or crafted.

When a Locked Vanishing Block has been right-clicked with a Tower Key, it will become an Unlocked Vanishing Block. However, these blocks do not vanish on their own; they need a neighbouring Vanishing Block in order to disappear.

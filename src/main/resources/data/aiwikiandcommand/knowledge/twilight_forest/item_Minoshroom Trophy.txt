The Minoshroom Trophy is a trophy rewarded after slaying the Minoshroom. Unlike most trophies, and alongside the Knight Phantom Trophy, these have an Iron-plated back, whereas the others are Gold-plated. When used in the Progression system, it is one of the Trophies usable on the Trophy Pedestal.

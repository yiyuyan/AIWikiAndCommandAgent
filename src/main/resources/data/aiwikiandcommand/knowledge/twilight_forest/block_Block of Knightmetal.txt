Block of Knightmetal is a block made from a grid of Knightmetal Ingots. One can efficiently store stacks of Knightmetal Ingots, stacking 9 into one block. Block of Knightmetal behave like a cactus, causing damage in 4 (♥ x 2) mob or player that touches it. Block of Knightmetal can also be used for crafting the Block and Chain weapon.

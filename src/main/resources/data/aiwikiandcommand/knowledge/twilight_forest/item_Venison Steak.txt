Venison Steak is made from cooking Raw Venison or slaying a Deer while it is on fire. It's a basic cooked food item that can be found quite easily, what more could one say? Despite being such a common staple of food within the Twilight Forest, it is easily one of the most nutritious. When eaten, Venison Steak will provide the player with a 8 () points of Hunger and 12.8 points of Saturation.

If all other food sources have been eaten, the player will be rewarded with the Advancement: We Dine At Eternal Sundown

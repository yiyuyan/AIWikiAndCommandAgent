Block of Fiery Metal is a block crafted from nine Fiery Ingots. This block does 1 () of fire damage every half second to mobs standing on top of it, and can therefore be used as an alternative to Magma Blocks. Like Netherrack and Magma Blocks, Block of Fiery Metal can infinitely sustain fire, and can keep Striders warm. Blocks of Fiery Metal can be crafted back into nine Fiery Ingots.

Like all other Twilight Forest storage blocks, it can be used as a Beacon base.

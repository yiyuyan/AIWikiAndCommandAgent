The Dense Twilight Forest biome is hillier and more thickly forested than normal Twilight Forest biome. This biome has more vegetation than most other biomes in the Twilight Forest, including more trees and bushes. The water is significantly darker than elsewhere in the forest. The Robust Twilight Oak Tree can be found as usual here, while Hollow Hills, Naga Courtyards, and Lich Towers may also be spotted nestled amongst the foliage.

The Dense Twilight Forest is unaffected by Progression and can be freely explored without having to defeat any bosses in the dimension.

The Enchanted Forest is a rare biome with distinct foliage. It is notable for the changing color of foliage, where rings of blue or green center on the middle of the biome. Much like the Dense Twilight Forest, the foliage here is quite thick, with large amounts of ferns covering the ground and vines adorning the leaves of trees. Rainbow Oak Trees spawn here in lieu of Canopy Trees, which can still be found but in fewer numbers. Robust Twilight Oak Trees also grow in this biome.

The Enchanted Forest is the only biome where it is possible to find the Quest Grove containing the Questing Ram, which is an even more valuable find as it poses the potential to receive one of each resource block for feeding the Questing Ram one of each color of wool.

Enchanted Forest is not affected by Progression and may be visited at any time.

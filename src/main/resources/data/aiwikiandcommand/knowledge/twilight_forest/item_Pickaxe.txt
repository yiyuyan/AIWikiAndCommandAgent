Pickaxes are a useful tool in mining Stone and ores, and a staple in cave exploration. The Twilight Forest adds a number of pickaxes, each with their own unique trait to them.

## Ironwood Pickaxe

The Ironwood Pickaxe is a tool made from Ironwood Ingots. When crafted, Ironwood Pickaxes have Efficiency I automatically enchanted. When used as a weapon, it has an attack damage of 4, similar to a Wooden Sword.

### Crafting

## Steeleaf Pickaxe

The Steeleaf Pickaxe is a tool made from Steeleaf. When crafted, Steeleaf Pickaxes have the Fortune II skill automatically enchanted. When used as a weapon, it has an attack damage of 5, similar to a Stone Sword.

### Crafting

## Knightmetal Pickaxe

The Knightmetal Pickaxe is a tool that can be used as a weapon, made from Knightmetal Ingots. As a tool, the Knightmetal Pickaxe can be used like a pickaxe. As a weapon, it has an attack rating of 5, similar to a Stone Sword, and an attack speed of 1.2, dealing extra damage to armored targets. One must note, however, that this is not a weapon like a sword, and will lose 2 durability points if used as a weapon.

### Crafting

## Fiery Pickaxe

The Fiery Pickaxe  is a tool made from Fiery Ingots. The Fiery Pickaxe does not come automatically enchanted when crafted, but has an innate ability of auto-smelting and setting fire to targets. When used as a weapon, it has an attack damage of 6, similar to an Iron Sword.

### Crafting

## Giant's Pickaxe

The Giant's Pickaxe is a tool that is dropped by Giants. The Giant's Pickaxe can be crafted, but is not automatically enchanted. However, the Giant's Pickaxe has the ability to break blocks of the same type in a 4x4x4 cube. If a 4x4x4 cube of stone is broken, instead of dropping 64 cobblestone, a piece of Giant Cobblestone drops instead. Users are warned that the durability of the Pickaxe depletes for every block broken (eg. If 4 blocks are broken by the Pickaxe, the durability falls by 4). Reach distance is also longer than a regular Pickaxe, increased by 2.5 blocks for both entities and blocks alike. When used as a weapon, it has an attack damage of 10, but the slow attack speed of 0.5. Giant's Pickaxe also has hitbox jitter beyond X/Z± 32,768.

The Dark Forest is a densely vegetated biome with a thick canopy that blocks almost all light from the forest floor. Darkwood Trees grow most commonly in this biome, with some smaller vegetation such as tall grass and flowers covering the grass. Occasionally, Mushglooms grow in the area, providing a faint source of light.

Despite the darkness, monsters such as Zombies and Skeletons will not spawn on the surface. However, other monsters such as Mist Wolves and King Spiders will spawn, making traversal in the Dark Forest more dangerous.

The Dark Forest will only generate around a Dark Forest Center.

## Progression
The Dark Forest is locked by Progression, creating a barrier of black tendrils that inflict the player with Darkness, making progression through the biome much more difficult.

In order to unlock the Dark Forest, the Twilight Lich must be defeated. This will clear the Darkness-inflicting effect as well as removing some structure protection from the Goblin Knight Stronghold.

As well as being locked by Progression, the Dark Forest is also key to progressing to the Dark Forest Center and Dark Tower by defeating the Knight Phantoms in the Goblin Knight Stronghold.

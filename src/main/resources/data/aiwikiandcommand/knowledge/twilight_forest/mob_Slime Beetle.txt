【生物信息】
类型：Hostile
生命值：25 (♥ x 12.5)
攻击力：8 (♥ x 4)
掉落：Slimeball (0-2)
ID：slime_beetle

Slime Beetles are slimy Beetles that can be found in the largest Hollow Hills and in Labyrinths. Slime Beetles fire green projectiles that look like Slimeballs. Be careful, they deal rather high damage and are fired with an incredible accuracy. They are also potentially hard to hit, as when you get very close to them, they retreat away from you.

Slime Beetles drop Slimeballs when killed.

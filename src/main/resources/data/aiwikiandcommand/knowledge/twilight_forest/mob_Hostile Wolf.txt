Hostile Wolves are strange Wolves that can only be found in a Hedge Maze. Legend says these Wolves hate humans because they always steal their treasures. Unfortunately, these Wolves cannot be tamed. However, if a player was to approach a Hostile Wolf in Creative Mode, it is likely they will spawn neutral and won't become hostile until they are attacked.

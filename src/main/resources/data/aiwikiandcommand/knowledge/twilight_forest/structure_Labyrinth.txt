The Labyrinth is a major landmark tied to Progression. It appears as an unremarkable small hill in the Twilight Swamp, but opens up to an entrance to an underground structure of sprawling maze passages. It contains two levels, each level appearing nearly as identical as each other, with the second floor encased in Bedrock.

The entire complex is constructed of Mazestone, an extremely hard material that dissuades players from digging holes through the walls, floor, and ceiling. Wooden arches and Iron Bar are the most common decorations found throughout the maze. Torches can be found occasionally scattered throughout the structure. Sometimes, a path may contain a small nook containing a barred-off Chest with loot, although some of these Chests will be trapped with TNT. Throughout the Labyrinth, there are several dead ends of notable features. Some dead ends will be filled with Torches, while others may contain water or lava streams. In lower levels, these dead ends may contain Huge Mushroom blocks.

A path may intersect with a large room. In the first floor, one of these rooms will contain a gate that blocks the second floor. Otherwise, these rooms may be completely empty, buried in Gravel or Huge Mushrooms, or contain several small barred columns with Chests or a monster spawner.

In the second level of the Labyrinth will be a room with fences blocking off the entryways. This room will contain the Minoshroom, as well as a variety of chests containing loot.

## Progression
The Labyrinth will be protected by a magical barrier that protects all blocks and monsters within the structure. In order to unlock the Labyrinth, the Twilight Lich must have been slain or have obtained a Twilight Lich Trophy or one of the four scepters. As part of progression, the previous advancements are also required.

Defeating the Minoshroom will not be enough to complete progression. Instead, a Meef Stroganoff must be consumed, granting the consumer immunity to the biome protection in the Fire Swamp as well as unlocking the Hydra Lair.

## Monsters
There are a variety of monsters that wander the corridors of the Labyrinth. Monsters such as the Creeper, Cave Spider, and Enderman can be found, as well as some other monsters endemic to the Twilight Forest, such as Fire Beetles, Slime Beetles, and Pinch Beetles.

Unique to the Labyrinth are two monsters: the Maze Slime and the Minotaur. When the Minoshroom is defeated, these monsters will no longer naturally spawn.

## Loot
There are various chests that can be found throughout the Labyrinth. The large rooms can have useful items such as Potions, as well as a vast array of Steeleaf equipment. These chests can also contain the Maze Map Focus, Charm of Keeping I, and Red Thread. Other loot that can be found include, Blaze Rod, Ironwood Ingots, and Steeleaf, as well as the Maze Wafer food item.

Hidden within the walls of the Labyrinth, a secret vault of rare and precious loot may be found. This room is trapped with TNT and Sand held above Pressure Plates. If successfully looted, items such as strongly enchanted Steeleaf equipment, Ender Chests, and Emeralds can be found. One of these four chests will also contain the Mazebreaker.

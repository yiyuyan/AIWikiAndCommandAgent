The Questing Ram Trophy is a trophy rewarded after completing the Questing Ram's quest. Unlike most trophies, these have an Ironwood-plated back, whereas the others are Gold-plated or Iron-plated. When used in the Progression system, it is one of the Trophies usable on the Trophy Pedestal, and can be obtained at any time.

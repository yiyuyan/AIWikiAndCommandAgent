Traveller's Vest is a piece of Traveller's Gear that can be worn in the Chest slot. Unlike most equipment, a Traveller's Vest will not break when durability is completely reduced. Instead, the ability and modifiers are rendered useless and it will no longer have any armor value.

When equipped, the Traveller's Vest comes with the Swift Swim ability, which provides a boost to swimming speed similar to Depth Strider.

## Crafting

This recipe accepts all Wool block colors.

## Modifiers
There are 6 additional modifiers that can be crafted into Traveller's Vest.

### Auto-Repair
This modifier allows for slow self-repair. The repair process does not require any materials and happens over time. In addition, repairing is sped up in direct sunlight, allowing for the gear to last longer. However, there is a 10 second cooldown for any possibility of self-repair after the armor piece has taken any durability damage which can lead to rapid decline in combat.

### Perfect Dodge
This modifier allows for avoiding projectiles entirely. When a projectile would make contact with the wearer, the projectile has a 30% chance to phase through them, protecting the wearer from all damage and the projectile to continue its trajectory.

### Stealth
This modifier allows for the wearer to turn invisible similar to the effects of Invisible. By crouching, the wearer will disappear from view, only showing equipped gear.

### Haste
This modifier provides the player with the Haste II effect, which increases mining and swing speed.

### Arrow Magnetism
This modifier automatically picks up any arrows the wearer shot but missed hitting an entity, regardless of distance from where the arrow lands. This works with all arrow types including tipped arrows.

### Efficient Eater
This modifier reduces the amount of hunger lost from moving around. This includes sprinting, swimming, jumping, and everything related to movement. It does not affect hunger lost from healing.

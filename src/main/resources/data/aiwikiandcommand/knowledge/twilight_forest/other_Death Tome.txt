Death Tomes are dangerous books float in the staircases and libraries of the Lich Tower. They fire a projectile that slows the creature they hit for a brief time. Death Tomes occasionally lose pages when struck.

They yield Paper, Books, and an occasional Book and Quill or Enchanted Book. If killed by the player, Death Tomes may also drop a Magic Map Focus upon death.

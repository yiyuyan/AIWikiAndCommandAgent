【方块信息】
hardness：1.5
tool：Shovel

Smoker is a decorative block that can be found in a Fire Swamp. These blocks appear to blend into the surroundings and emit a constant stream of smoke, though this smoke has no other effect. Smokers can be harvested with a Shovel, and are primarily used to craft Encased Smokers.

Nagastone Stairs are a unique block that can be found in the Naga Courtyard. These blocks have two variants: Left and Right. Depending on how the block was crafted will yield different results, either the swirl patterns will point left or right.

## Nagastone Stairs (Left)
【方块信息】
hardness：1.5
tool：Pickaxe

Nagastone Stairs (Left) are the left variant of Nagastone Stairs, with the swirl patterns facing left.

### Crafting

## Cracked Nagastone Stairs (Left)
【方块信息】
hardness：1.5
tool：Pickaxe

Cracked Nagastone Stairs (Left) are a variant of the left-facing Nagastone Stairs. This block has cracks all over the surface, as if it were eroded over time.

### Crafting

## Mossy Nagastone Stairs (Left)
【方块信息】
hardness：1.5

Mossy Nagastone Stairs (Left) are a variant of the left-facing Nagastone Stairs. This block has patches of moss growing all over the surface, as if the block is being reclaimed by nature.

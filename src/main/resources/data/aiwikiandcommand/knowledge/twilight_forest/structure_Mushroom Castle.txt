You may find castles made of Giant Mushroom Blocks when exploring Dense Mushroom Forests. These castles are Mushroom Castles, and stand tall over the biome. They have no entrances and nothing in them. They are perfect places to store stuff, as no mobs spawn there and there is no boss to conquer.

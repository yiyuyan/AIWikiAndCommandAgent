Liveroot is an item that can be harvested from Liveroots. Liveroot contains a potent energy, but it requires additional materials to become active. For one to activate the magical properties in Liveroot, one must combine it with an Iron Ingot and a Gold Nugget to create Raw Ironwood.

## Composting
Placing Liveroot into a Composter has a 50% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

Encased Fire Jet is a functional block variant of the Fire Jet. Where a Fire Jet will operate independently, Encased Fire Jets will only operate on a Redstone signal. When a signal is provided, the Encased Fire Jet will open up and activate, emitting a few embers before emitting a large stream of fire. Encased Fire Jet can be harvested with an Axe.

Encased Fire Jet has four states. The first state is the Idle state, which will only occur when a Redstone signal is not present. Once a signal is provided, the Encased Fire Jet will enter the Popping state, emitting small embers for a couple seconds. Once enough time has passed, the Encased Fire Jet will enter the Flame state, emitting a large stream of fire. Unlike Fire Jets, Encased Fire Jets do not need a Lava source under the block to burn. When the eruption has stopped, the Encased Fire Jet will enter a Timeout state and will remain until the signal is disabled where it will enter Idle again.

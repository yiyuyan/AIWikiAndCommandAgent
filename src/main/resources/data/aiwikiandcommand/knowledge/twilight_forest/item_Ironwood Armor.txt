Ironwood Armor is an armor set made from Ironwood Ingots. This armor set is the first armor set an adventurer is most likely to find or craft within the Twilight Forest.

__NOTOC__

## Ironwood Helmet

The Ironwood Helmet is a part of the Ironwood Armor set, used in the Helmet slot. When crafted, Ironwood Helmet is automatically enchanted with the Aqua Affinity enchant. When worn, it has an armor rating of 2, similar to the Iron Helmet.

### Crafting

## Ironwood Chestplate

The Ironwood Chestplate is a part of the Ironwood Armor set, used in the Chestplate slot. When crafted, Ironwood Chestplate is automatically enchanted with the Protection I enchant. When worn, it has an armor rating of 7, stronger than the Iron Chestplate.

### Crafting

## Ironwood Leggings

The Ironwood Leggings is a part of the Ironwood Armor set, used in the Leggings slot. When crafted, Ironwood Leggings is automatically enchanted with the Protection I enchant. When worn, it has an armor rating of 5, similar to the Iron Leggings.

### Crafting

## Ironwood Boots

The Ironwood Boots is a part of the Ironwood Armor set, used in the Boots slot. When crafted, Ironwood Boots is automatically enchanted with the Feather Falling I enchant. When worn it has an armor rating of 2, similar to the Iron Boots.

Like the Overworld, the Twilight Forest is populated with a variety of trees. Most trees have saplings and can be obtained from decaying leaves or from Leaf Dungeons.

## Sickly Twilight Oak Sapling
【方块信息】
hardness：0
tool：Any Tool
transparent：Yes

The Sickly Twilight Oak Sapling is a sapling that grows into a Twilight Oak Tree. This sapling can be found commonly in Leaf Dungeons or dropped by Twilight Oak Leaves.

## Darkwood Sapling
【方块信息】
hardness：0
tool：Any Tool
transparent：Yes

The Darkwood Sapling is a sapling that grows into a Darkwood Tree. This sapling can be found commonly in Leaf Dungeons. These sapling are imbued with strange magic that prevents it from growing below the y-level of 30.

## Robust Twilight Oak Sapling
【方块信息】
hardness：0
tool：Any Tool
transparent：Yes

The Robust Twilight Oak Sapling is a sapling that grows into a Giant Twilight Oak Tree. Once it grows,  Fireflies and Cicadas will generate with the tree, along with the occasional Leaf Dungeon. This sapling can be found sometimes in Leaf Dungeons.

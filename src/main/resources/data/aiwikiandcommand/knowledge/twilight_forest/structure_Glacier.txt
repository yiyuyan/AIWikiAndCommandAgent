Glaciers are gigantic slabs of ice that are several hundred meters wide and long and several dozen meters tall. The ice is easily spotted through the Snowy Forest, towering as high as the fir trees surrounding it. It is little wonder that the only life to be found in these bitterly cold conditions are the colonies of Penguins that dot the empty landscape, though where they get their food from in such barren conditions is a mystery perhaps better left to other, more qualified explorers.

Adventurers traversing the ice can expect to find the forbidding and dangerous Aurora Palace, standing proud and with a haunting beauty with its ever-shifting kaleidoscope of colours.

## Progression
Although defeating the Twilight Lich will lift the blizzard howling through the snowy firs, it seems, if anything, even more ferocious on top of the glacier itself. Adventurers who defeat the Alpha Yeti will find that just touching his downy fur will transfer a portion of the magical power from the fur to themselves, dispelling the winds and leaving the Palace open for exploration.

【生物信息】
类型：Passive
生命值：10 (♥♥♥♥♥)
攻击力：None
掉落：Raw Porkchop (1-3)
ID：boar

Wild Boars, despite their tusks, will not attempt to attack the player. Wild Boars are the Twilight forest eqivalent of a pig, and can be saddled and ridden like a Pig. Controls are the same as Pigs, with the use of a Carrot on a Stick. Like Pigs, they are somewhat shy and not aggressive. Wild Boars drop 1-2 Raw Porkchop when killed or Cooked Porkchop if it was on fire. Wild Boars can also be bred with Carrots or Potatos.

Kobolds are small, cobalt-blue creatures with large ears. They are found almost anywhere that is dark and tend to group into large packs.

Kobolds drop Wheat and Gold Nugget on death.

In Progression locked areas, a Kobold may appear holding a Signed Book that drops it on death. This book gives a hint on how to unlock the area.

Killing a Kobold while in a pack will cause nearby Kobolds to panic quickly for a few seconds. Kobolds can also be distracted by dropping Bread. They will pick up the bread and run off while eating it.

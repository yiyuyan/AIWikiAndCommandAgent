Maze Wafer is a special food item that can be commonly found within chests in a Labyrinth or an Aurora Palace. This food item is a welcoming sight for adventurers, as exploring these structures can quickly drain their resources, and a constant supply of Maze Wafers is more than welcome to the eyes of many. When eaten, Maze Wafers will provide the player with 4 () points of Hunger and 4.8 points of Saturation.

If all other food sources have been eaten, the player will be rewarded with the Advancement: We Dine At Eternal Sundown

## Composting
Placing a Maze Wafer into a Composter has a 65% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

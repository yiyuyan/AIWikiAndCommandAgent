Nether Berries are the colloquial term for berries that can be found in the Dark Tower. Despite their name, Nether Berries do not naturally generate in the Nether.

Unlike Overworld Berries, Nether Berries have additional effects when consumed. The length of time granted by consuming a Nether Berry stacks, so the more berries are consumed, the longer the effect lasts. These effects can be beneficial, but there is also a chance that a negative status effect is applied. As such, caution should be taken when consuming a Nether Berry.

## Blightberry

Blightberry is a food item obtained from Blightberry Bushes. It can restore 1 () point of hunger and has a saturation modifier of 0.4.

When consumed, the Blightberry will apply 8 seconds of Regeneration. However, there is a 75% chance that Posion is applied for 5 seconds, and a 15% chance Wither is applied for 5 seconds.

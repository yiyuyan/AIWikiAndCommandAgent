Nestled among the hills and fir trees of the Snowy Forest, the Yeti Cave appears as a square-shaped hill. Four entrances allow access to the ice-covered insides, where packed ice hangs from stalactites and covers the floor. Many fierce Yetis make their home in these frigid caves, lorded over by an Alpha Yeti.

## Progression
A magical blizzard howls through the Snowy Forest, freezing anyone venturing in. The lair offers no protection against this unnatural weather, as well as protecting the Yetis from any attempts to harm them. Only by defeating a Twilight Lich will the blizzard cease.

Vanquishing the Alpha Yeti and harvesting some of his luxurious fur will allow an explorer to access the Glacier and the Aurora Palace that stands atop it.

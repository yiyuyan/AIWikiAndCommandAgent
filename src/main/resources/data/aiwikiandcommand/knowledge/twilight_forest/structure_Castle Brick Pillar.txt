Decorating the Final Castle are decorative blocks called Castle Pillars. Like all other Castle Bricks, if these blocks are broken, durability is severely reduced by 17. There are two variants of Castle Pillar, both of which can be crafted or stonecut.

## Bold Castle Brick Pillar
【方块信息】
hardness：100
tool：Diamond Pickaxe

Bold Castle Brick Pillar is a type of Castle Pillar that generates in the Final Castle. These blocks have a slightly darker emboss though the middle of the pillar on the sides, while the tops and bottoms have a lighter square in the cross-pattern. These blocks generate inside of the Final Castle, standing as large pillars and generate along the bridges, acting like fences. Bold Castle Brick Pillar can be used to craft Bold Castle Brick Tiles or Bold Castle Brick Stairs.

## Encased Castle Brick Pillar
【方块信息】
hardness：100
tool：Diamond Pickaxe

Encased Castle Brick Pillar is a type of Castle Pillar that does not currently generate in the Final Castle. These blocks have a black emboss though the middle of the pillar on the sides, while the tops and bottoms have a cross-pattern. Encased Castle Brick Pillar can be used to craft itself, Encased Castle Brick Tiles or Encased Castle Brick Stairs.

Swords are the main weapon for melee damage. The Twilight Forest has plenty of swords to use, and they all have an attack speed of 1.6, except Giant's Sword.

## Ironwood Sword

The Ironwood Sword is a weapon made from Ironwood Ingots. When crafted, the Ironwood Sword will be enchanted with the Knockback I enchantment. Ironwood Sword has a damage rating of 6, similar to that of an Iron Sword.

### Crafting

## Steeleaf Sword

The Steeleaf Sword is a weapon made from Steeleaf. When crafted, the Steeleaf Sword will be enchanted with the Looting II enchantment. Steeleaf Sword has a damage rating of 7, similar to that of a Diamond Sword.

### Crafting

## Knightmetal Sword

The Knightmetal Sword is a weapon made from Knightmetal Ingots. Knightmetal Swords are not enchanted when crafted, but is able to deal extra damage to targets with armor. Knightmetal Sword has a damage rating of 7, similar to that of a Diamond Sword.

### Crafting

## Fiery Sword

The Fiery Sword is a weapon made from Fiery Ingots. When crafted, the Fiery Sword will be enchanted with the Fire Aspect II enchantment. Fiery Sword has a damage rating of 8, stronger than a Diamond Sword.

### Crafting

## Ice Sword

The Ice Sword is a weapon that can be found in Aurora Palaces. Ice Swords cannot be crafted, and inflict 10 seconds of  Frosted III on targets, reducing their movement speed. Ice Sword has a damage rating of 7.5, slightly stronger than a Diamond Sword, but one must be careful due to the low durability. It can be repaired in an Anvil with Packed Ice.

## Glass Sword

The Glass Sword is a weapon that can occasionally be found in Aurora Palaces. Glass Swords cannot be crafted, and have no skills. Glass Sword has the highest damage rating of all swords in the Twilight Forest, rating at 40 damage. Adventurers are warned: once the Glass Sword makes contact with the target, it will smash to pieces. However, enchanting this sword with Unbreaking gives a chance for it to be used more than once.

## Giant's Sword

The Giant's Sword is a weapon that is dropped by Armored Giants. Giant's Swords can be crafted as well, and the reach distance is longer than a regular sword, giving a bonus 2.5 block and entity reach. Unlike the other swords, Giant's Sword has the attack speed of 0.5 and damage rating of 12.

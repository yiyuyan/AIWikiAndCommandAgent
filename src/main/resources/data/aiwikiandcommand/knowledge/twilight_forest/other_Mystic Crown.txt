Mystic Crown is a special item crafted from Crown Splinters. It can be equipped in the helmet slot, offering only 2 points of armor. It cannot be enchanted, but it has no durability.

When equipped, the powers of the Scepters are enhanced, providing more benefits from each use.
* Twilight Scepter: The scepter has a 5% chance to not consume a charge.
* Scepter of Life Draining: The scepter has a 5% chance to not consume a charge.
* Zombie Scepter: The scepter has a 10% chance to spawn a baby Loyal Zombie.
* Scepter of Fortification: The shields granted to the wearer will last 50% longer.

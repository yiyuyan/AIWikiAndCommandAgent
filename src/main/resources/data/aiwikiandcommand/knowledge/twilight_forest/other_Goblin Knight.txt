The Goblin Knight is not only a single enemy, but two goblins stacking up together. Attacking the behind of a Goblin Knight removes the armor, revealing the two stacked Goblins.

## Upper Goblin Knight
【生物信息】
类型：Hostile
生命值：30 (♥ x 15)
攻击力：2 (♥)
掉落：Armor Shard (0-2)
ID：upper_goblin_knight

The upper-half of the Goblin Knight has more health than the lower-half. This half wears a helmet and holds a shield and spear. The shield protects the front of the Goblin Knight and negates any damage. The shield can be destroyed by defeating the lower-half of the Goblin Knight first or attacking the shield-side of the upper-half of the Goblin Knight. When close to the upper-half's front, whether its attached to the lower-half or not, the Goblin Knight will stop moving and raise its spear above the player for a few seconds before striking it to the ground. If this attack hits you then it will deal enough damage to instantly kill you. Hitting the upper-half with an axe will temporarily disable its shield and leave it vulnerable similar to a regular player shield. Slaying the Upper Goblin Knight leaves the Lower Goblin Knight vulnerable.

## Lower Goblin Knight
【生物信息】
类型：Hostile
生命值：20 (♥ x 10)
攻击力：2 (♥)
掉落：Armor Shard (0-2)
ID：lower_goblin_knight

The lower-half of the Goblin Knight has less health than the upper-half. This half only controls the movement of the Goblin Knight, and if the upper-half is slain, will begin to attack. Slaying the Lower Goblin Knight leaves the Upper Goblin Knight behind, only wielding a spear and losing it's shield.

Bighorn Sheep are the Twilight Forest equivalent of the mundane Sheep. They can be slain or sheared for Wool, and Mutton or Cooked Mutton if slain while on fire. The color of the wool dropped depends on the initial colour of the Bighorn Sheep. Bighorn Sheep appear in a variety of colours, where Brown Bighorn Sheep are the most common, but other colours can occur, though not as frequently.

Similar to their otherworldly counterpart, naming a Bighorn Sheep "jeb_" will cause it to shift it's colours constantly. If by chance an Evoker were to ever come across a Blue Bighorn Sheep, it will change it's colour to red.

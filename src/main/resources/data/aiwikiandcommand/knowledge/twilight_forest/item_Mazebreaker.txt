The Mazebreaker is a special kind of Pickaxe only found in Labyrinth treasure vaults, viewable with a Maze Map. The Mazebreaker can mine through all types of Mazestone while only losing one durability point, whereas breaking Mazestone with any other pickaxe will lower that pickaxe's durability 16x faster. By default, the Mazebreaker is enchanted with Efficiency IV, Unbreaking III and Fortune II.

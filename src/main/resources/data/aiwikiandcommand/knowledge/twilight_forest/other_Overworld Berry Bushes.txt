Overworld Berry Bushes are the colloquial term for berry bushes that can be found throughout Twilight Forest and in Camps. An Overworld Berry Bush has 4 growth stages, where the last stage bears fruit. Once a berry bush has been harvested, it will slowly regrow more berries.

In order for an Overworld Berry Bush to grow, it will need to be planted on natural ground such such as Dirt, Grass Block, or Mud. To grow, the berry bush will need to be in a light level of 8 or more.

## Raspberry Bush
【方块信息】
hardness：0.4
tool：Shears
luminance：0

Raspberry Bush is a block that grows in the Twilight Forest. It can be used to obtain Raspberries.

A Raspberry Bush can be found in a Camp or randomly in Dense Twilight Forest, Clearing, or Mushroom Forest. Rarely, a Raspberry Bush can also be found in a Twilight Swamp.

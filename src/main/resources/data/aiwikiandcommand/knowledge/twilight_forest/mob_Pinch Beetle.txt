Pinch Beetles are frightening Beetles that can be found in the largest Hollow Hills, Labyrinths, and in Dark Towers. Pinch Beetles charge at you and grab your body, hoisting you into the air between its pincers. While holding onto you like this, they will continually deal damage until either you or the Pinch Beetle dies. Pinch Beetles are very fast and are extremely dangerous near lava, a cliff, or other enemies.

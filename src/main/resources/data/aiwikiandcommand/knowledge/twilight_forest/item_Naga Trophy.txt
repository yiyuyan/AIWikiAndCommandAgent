The Naga Trophy is a decorative human head-like trophy that is dropped after slaying a Naga. It appears as a much smaller version of the Naga's head and can be placed on any block. If using the Progression system, once the Naga has been slain, it can be used to unlock the Trophy Pedestal in the Goblin Knight Stronghold.

As befits their magical properties, each type of Magic Tree have their own kind of special leaves, animated with strange patterns and varying shades of colour depending on where the tree was planted in the world. Like other leaf blocks, Shears can be used to harvest the decorative leaves if so desired.

Placing Magic Tree Leaves into a Composter has a 30% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

__TOC__

## Timewood Leaves
Timewood Leaves are leaves that grow from the Tree of Time. These leaves vary their colour from a dull green to a deep red colour.

## Transwood Leaves
Leaves of Transformation are leaves that grow from the Tree of Transformation. These leaves vary their colour from cyan to grey. Leaves of Transformation are special in that they emit particles similar to an Enchanting Table, leaking from the leaves that animate with the same enchanting pattern.

## Minewood Leaves
Miner's Leaves are leaves that grow from the Miner's Tree. These leaves vary their colour from yellow to gold.

## Sortingwood Leaves
Sorting Leaves are leaves that grow from Sorting Trees. These leaves vary their colour from lime green to dark green, with a continuous pattern of arrows flowing through the branches.

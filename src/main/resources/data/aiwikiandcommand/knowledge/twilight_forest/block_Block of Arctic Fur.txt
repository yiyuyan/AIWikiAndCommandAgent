Block of Arctic Fur is a block crafted from nine Arctic Fur. This block is mostly decorative, used to store numerous pieces of Arctic Fur into one block. If one was to land on a Block of Arctic Fur from a tall height, they will receive a 90% reduction in fall damage. Block of Arctic Fur can be crafted back into nine Arctic Fur pieces.

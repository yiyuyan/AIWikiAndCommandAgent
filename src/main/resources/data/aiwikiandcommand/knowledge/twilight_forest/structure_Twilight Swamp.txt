Humid and uncomfortable, the Twilight Swamp is a relatively uncommon sight in the Twilight Forest, and shares many similarities with swamps found in the Overworld. Due to the wet and muddy conditions, instead of Canopy Trees, expect to find Mangrove Trees and smaller swamp oak trees, many of which have long creeping vines reaching to the ground. The Robust Twilight Oak Trees do manage to survive here, towering over the ponds. Many pools of water can be found here, with an abundance of lily pads of various sizes. Mosquito Swarms are frequently encountered here, and can leave an unprepared traveller sickened with a wasting hunger.

For reasons unknown, the territorial Minotaurs have chosen to construct their labyrinthine homes beneath the swamp, with other creatures joining with them in the twisting darkness. The entrances to the Labyrinths can be discerned fairly easily by the experienced adventurer, as they resemble small Hollow Hills with entrances in the four cardinal directions, with the shaft into the maze below at the centre.

## Progression
Although mosquitos are predominant throughout the swamps, they seem to be in biblical plague proportions when first approaching the Swamp, making it impossible to proceed into the biome without being attacked with the hunger sickness. Finding and killing a Twilight Lich in its tower appears to be the only way to disperse the clouds of insects.

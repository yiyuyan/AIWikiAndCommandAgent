Mushgloom is a decorative block that can be found in various gloomy places such as caves and certain biomes. These mushrooms can be harvested without any tool for placement in other locations. They do not require tilled soil, and can be placed on any dirt block. Mushgloom can also be planted in a Flower Pot.

Although Mushgloom does not respond to Bone Meal, if it is placed on Uberous Soil, it can grow into a Huge Mushgloom.

Placing a Mushgloom into a Composter has a 65% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

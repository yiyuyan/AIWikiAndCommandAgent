The Final Castle is a large structure that spawns in the Final Plateau. It is mostly comprised of white castle bricks of different variants. Some castle bricks are engraved with different colored runes and covered with force fields.

Presently, the Final Castle is complete, with no active achievements for the castle. No mobs spawn inside of the castle. This is intended by the developers.

## Progression
Once the player has traversed the Thornlands, they will come across the Final Castle, where the Final Boss will never be located.

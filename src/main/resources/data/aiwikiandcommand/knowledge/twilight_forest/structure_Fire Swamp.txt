The Fire Swamp can be found in the middle of the Twilight Swamp.

Lava Pools, Smokers, and Fire Jets can be found here. The Fire Swamp is the natural home of the Hydra in the Hydra Lair.

## Progression
Defeating the Minoshroom at the bottom of the Labyrinth and consuming Meef Stroganoff will fortify the player against the burning ash, allowing exploration into the Fire Swamp.

Otherwise, players will be set on fire for 8 seconds every 3 seconds.

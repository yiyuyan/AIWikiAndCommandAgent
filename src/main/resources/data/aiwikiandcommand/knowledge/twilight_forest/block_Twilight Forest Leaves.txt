The Twilight Forest is home to many trees, and with these trees are leaves. There are several variants of Twilight Forest Leaves.

## Twilight Oak Leaves
【方块信息】
hardness：0.2
tool：Shears
flammable：Yes
transparent：Partial

Twilight Oak Leaves are leaves that grow from Twilight Oak Trees and Robust Twilight Oak Trees. These leaves appear similar to the Oak Leaves that grow on Oak Trees.

## Canopy Tree Leaves
【方块信息】
hardness：0.2
tool：Shears
flammable：Yes
transparent：Partial

Canopy Tree Leaves are leaves that grow from Canopy Trees. These leaves appear similar to the Spruce Leaves that grow on Spruce Trees, except with a greener tint.

## Mangrove Leaves
【方块信息】
hardness：0.2
tool：Shears
flammable：Yes
transparent：Partial

Mangrove Tree Leaves are leaves that grow from Mangrove Trees. These leaves appear similar to the Birch Leaves that grow on Birch Trees, except with a brighter tint.

## Darkwood Leaves
【方块信息】
hardness：2
tool：Shears
flammable：Yes
transparent：Partial

Darkwood Leaves are leaves that grow from Darkwood Trees. These leaves are special as they block out light beneath it and has more blast resistance than most leaves. Perhaps from magic, Darkwood Leaves are flammable, but it won't spread, which could explain why the forest doesn't burn down when there are pools of lava on the trees.

## Rainbow Oak Leaves
【方块信息】
hardness：0.2
tool：Shears
flammable：Yes
transparent：Partial

Rainbow Oak Leaves are leaves that grow from Rainbow Oak Trees. These leaves appear similar to the Oak Leaves that grow on Oak Trees, except with a rainbow color, based on where it is placed in the color map.

## Thorn Leaves
【方块信息】
hardness：0.2
tool：Shears
flammable：Yes
transparent：Partial

Thorn Leaves are leaves that grow from random Thorns. These leaves appear similar to Spurce Leaves that grow on Spruce Trees.

## Beanstalk Leaves
【方块信息】
hardness：0.2
tool：Shears
flammable：Yes
transparent：Partial

Beanstalk Leaves are leaves that grow from Beanstalks. These leaves appear similar to Azalea Leaves that grow on Azalea Trees. Breaking these leaves may drop Magic Beans.

## Composting
All Leaves have a 30% chance of raising the compost level by 1, expect beanstalk leaves, which have a 50% chance.

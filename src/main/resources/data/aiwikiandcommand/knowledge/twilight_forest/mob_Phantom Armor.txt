Phantom Armor is a special set of armor that can be obtained after slaying a Knight Phantom. There is only two parts to this set, and has greater durability then all other armor sets, along with an armor toughness rating of 2.5. This armor is also unique in that it will not be lost on death, and cannot be enchanted with Curse of Vanishing or Curse of Binding.

__NOTOC__

## Phantom Helmet

The Phantom Helmet is a part of the Phantom Armor set, used in the Helmet slot. Phantom Helmet cannot be crafted, but if it is picked up from a chest after slaying all Knight Phantoms, it will be strongly enchanted. When worn, it has an armor rating of 3, similar to the Diamond Helmet.

## Phantom Chestplate

The Phantom Chestplate is a part of the Phantom Armor set, used in the Chestplate slot. Phantom Chestplate cannot be crafted, but if it is picked up from a chest after slaying all Knight Phantoms, it will be strongly enchanted. When worn, it has an armor rating of 8, similar to the Diamond Chestplate.

【物品信息】
durability：99

The Scepter of Life Draining is a scepter dropped by the Twilight Lich which drains the life energy of whatever mob is right-clicked.

On using a Scepter of Life Draining, a line of pink particles will connect to the target entity and slowly damage it. In turn, the Scepter of Life Draining will also heal the user if successfully damaged. If the transfer of life energy is stopped midway through, the enemy will have the Slowness debuff applied to them. The scepter does not work against entities marked as a boss.

The Scepter of Life Draining holds 99 charges and can be recharged by placing an empty Scepter of Life Draining with a Fermented Spider Eye or an Exanimate Essence in a crafting table. Recharging will only restore 9 charges. The Scepter will never break once it's depleted. It can only be enchanted with Renewal.

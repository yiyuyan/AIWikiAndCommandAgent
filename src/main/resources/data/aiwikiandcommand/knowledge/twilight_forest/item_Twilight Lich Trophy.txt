The Twilight Lich Trophy is a decorative humanoid trophy that is dropped after defeating a Lich. The Twilight Lich Trophy appears as almost the exact size as the Twilight Lich's head and can be placed on any block. In the Progression system, the Twilight Lich Trophy is one of the trophies that can be used on a Trophy Pedestal in the Goblin Knight Stronghold.

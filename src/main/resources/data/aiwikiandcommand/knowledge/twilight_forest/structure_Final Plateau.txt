The Final Plateau is a tall, bare biome. Where almost all biomes in the Twilight Forest contain all kinds of foliage, the Final Plateau is completely bare of vegetation barring some Thorns from the neighboring Thornlands. Instead, the surface of the biome consists of a stony block called Deadrock, and no other natural features generate. Aside from the odd Raven, no mobs naturally spawn in the biome.

## Progression
The Final Plateau is locked by Progression, creating a constant downpour of acid rain that chips away at the player's health. It is not possible to shelter from the effect.

In order to unlock the Final Plateau, a Lamp of Cinders must be acquired from the vault in the Troll Caves, dispelling the rain and assisting in the traversal of the thorny barrier.

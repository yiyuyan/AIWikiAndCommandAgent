Ironwood Ingot is an item that can be obtained from smelting Raw Ironwood. Ironwood Ingots can be used similar to iron or other materials to make tools and armor, as well as its own storage block. The tools and armor crafted with Ironwood Ingots come pre-imbued with enchantments.

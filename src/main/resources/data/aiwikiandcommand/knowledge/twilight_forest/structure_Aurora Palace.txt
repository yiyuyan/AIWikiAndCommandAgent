The Aurora Palace is a structure found in the Glacier biome. It is comprised of Aurora Blocks that swirl between green and blue colors and various rooms that are stacked and branching from each other. This structure is home to the Snow Queen, the major boss of the Snow Forest branch of progression.

The overall structure of the palace consists of multiple tall towers, interconnected by small doorways at varying levels within the chambers. Some rooms can be reached by simply walking up, others require platforming to reach the next room. Inside the palace are various rooms comprised of more Aurora Blocks, as well as columns of Aurora Pillars and various rooms contain Birch Planks and Slabs. One room will be coated in Packed Ice and Ice where the Snow Queen resides. Traversing this room will require breaking the ice blocks.

## Progression
The Aurora Palace will be protected by a magical barrier that protects all blocks and monsters within the structure. In order to unlock the Aurora Palace, the Alpha Yeti must have been slain or have obtained an Alpha Yeti Trophy or Alpha Yeti Fur. As part of progression, the previous advancements are also required.

Defeating the Snow Queen ends the Snow Forest part of the Twilight Forest journey. In order to explore the Twilight Highlands, the Hydra and Ur-Ghast must be defeated.

## Monsters
The Aurora Palace contains a variety of monsters unique to this structure, while other monsters such as Zombies and Skeletons are less common. Stable Ice Cores are monsters that shoot icy projectiles at any target, while Unstable Ice Cores will swarm around targets where they will begin a detonation process when damaged. Another enemy exclusive to the Aurora Palace are Snow Guardians, often seen as floating sets of armor and equipped with a matching weapon.

## Loot
Chests are usually to be found hidden amongst the pillars in the palace. Some of these chests contain food like Maze Wafers and Arrow, useful for the travels ahead, but others contain valuable loot such as Ironwood Ingots, Steeleaf and Charms of Keeping.

There is also loot exclusive to the Aurora Palace. Found in specific rooms are one of a Glass Sword, Ice Bow, or Ender Bow.

Borer Essence is an item that is dropped after killing a Towerwood Borer, each dropping 0-2. Alternatively, you can come across a room with item frames that display the recipe for Carminite that provides you with 4 essence free. Borer Essence is used is to craft Carminite and upgrade Traveller's Wings.

Spiral Bricks is a block that can be found in the Naga Courtyard. This block has a unique appearance, designed to be arranged in a 2x2 vertical arrangement, while the flat sides of the block resemble the face of Chiseled Stone Bricks. Spiral Bricks are crafted from 5 Stone or Stone Bricks and 4 Stone or Stone Brick Slabs.

## Crafting

Stone Bricks can be replaced with Cracked Stone Bricks, Mossy Stone Bricks, or Chiselled Stone Bricks

Stone Slabs can be replaced with Stone Brick Slabs

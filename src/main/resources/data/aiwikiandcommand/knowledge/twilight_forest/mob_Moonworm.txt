Moonworms are critter blocks fired from from Moonworm Queens, either by using it on a surface or launching them. Once a Moonworm Queen creates a Moonworm, it can be destroyed and moved. A Firefly can be worn in the Helmet slot, though it offers no additional protection and it does not emit light.

When broken, the Moonworm will drop itself. However, when shot with an Arrow or crushed with an Anvil, it will drop Dye.

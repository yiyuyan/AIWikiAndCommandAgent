Iron Ladders are blocks that can be found in some variants of Druid Huts, mimicing the functions of a Ladder, but being much stronger due to it being made out of Iron. It is possible to craft and obtain these ladders to make for your own home! Iron Ladders, when placed next to one another, will extend their sides to the furthest left and right ladder, connecting all inner ladders into one singular beam.

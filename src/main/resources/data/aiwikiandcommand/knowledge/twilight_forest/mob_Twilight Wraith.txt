Twilight Wraiths are floating spirits occasionally found in the largest of the Hollow Hills, as well as the interior of other dungeons occasionally. They appear as dark, ghostly, mostly transparent creatures wearing a thin gold crown. These enemies will fly around, gazing at you and damage you if you come close enough. Sometimes, a Wraith will chase you down. Because of this, it is recommended to bring a bow to slay a Wraith.

Twilight Wraiths drop Glowstone Dust when slain.

The Labyrinths of the Twilight Forest are made of this blue-grey stone which comes in several variations. Mazestone is extremely hard to break, with twice the breaking resistance of obsidian. Unlike obsidian, however, Mazestone has weak blast resistance, and the various explosive creatures and traps in the Labyrinths will readily demolish large sections of the walls.

Those attempting to mine through the maze will notice that Mazestone wears down pickaxes at 16 times the normal rate.

## Variants

### Mazestone
Mazestone is a block found in the Labyrinth. This block is mostly bare and unpatterned, similar to Smooth Sandstone. Mazestone is mainly found on the roof of the Labyrinth, as well as under the hedges in the Hedge Maze.

### Mazestone Brick
Mazestone Brick is a block found in the Labyrinth. This block is patterned like bricks. Mazestone Brick is mainly found on the walls of the Labyrinth, typically mixed in with Cracked and Mossy Mazestone.

### Cracked Mazestone Brick
Cracked Mazestone Brick is a block found in the Labyrinth. This block looks like Mazestone Bricks, but a cracked variant. Cracked Mazestone Brick is mainly found on the walls of the Labyrinth, typically mixed in with Mossy Mazestone Brick and Mazestone Brick.

### Mossy Mazestone Brick
Mossy Mazestone Brick is a block found in the Labyrinth. This block is patterned like bricks, but with moss strewn through the gaps.

### Cut Mazestone
Cut Mazestone is a block found in the Labyrinth. This block looks similar to Cut Sandstone. Cut Mazestone is infrequently seen in the maze, only appearing on the corners of the walls.

### Decorative Mazestone Brick
Decorative Mazestone Brick is a block found in the Labyrinth. This block is quite detailed, with several marking decorated on the sides. Decorative Mazestone Brick is mainly found on the walls of the Labyrinth, lining the tops and bottoms.

### Mazestone Mosaic
Mazestone Mosaic is a block found in the Labyrinth. This block is patterned on the top and bottom of the block with a circular pattern. Mazestone Mosaic can be found decorating the ground of the Labyrinth.

### Mazestone Border
Mazestone Border is a block found in the Labyrinth. This block is patterned on the top and bottom of the block with a square pattern. Mazestone Border can only be found in the larger rooms in the Labyrinth, outlining the room.

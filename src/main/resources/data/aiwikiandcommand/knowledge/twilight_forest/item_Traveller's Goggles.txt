Traveller's Goggles are a piece of Traveller's Gear that can be worn in the Helmet slot. Unlike most equipment, Traveller's Goggles will not break when durability is completely reduced. Instead, the ability and modifiers are rendered useless and it will no longer have any armor value.

When equipped, the Traveller's Goggles comes with the Zoom ability, which can provide an innate zoom function. By holding down the Z key, the wearer can zoom their view in, similar in function to the Spyglass.

As the Traveller's Goggles requires Gold Ingots in the crafting recipe, wearing Traveller's Goggles can prevent Piglin hostility.

## Modifiers
There are 5 additional modifiers that can be added to Traveller's Goggles.

### Auto-Repair
This modifier allows for slow self-repair. The repair process does not require any materials and happens over time. In addition, repairing is sped up in direct sunlight, allowing for the gear to last longer. However, there is a 10 second cooldown for any possibility of self-repair after the armor piece has taken any durability damage which can lead to rapid decline in combat.

### Red Thread Vision
This modifier adds the ability to view Red Thread without the need to hold onto the item. A keybind can be set to toggle this effect off and on.

### All-Night Goggles
This modifier affects certain player functions regarding vision at night. When worn, Phantom will not spawn around the wearer and will pause the insomnia timer to further prevent Phantom spawning. In addition, it also stops Endermen from attacking the wearer when looked at.

### Item Display
This modifier lets certain items to be inserted into the Traveller's Goggles. Items can be inserted via the same functionality as a Bundle by right-clicking specific items on the Goggles or the Goggles onto items. Four different items are currently supported:
* Adding a Compass will display the player's coordinates.
* Adding a Clock will display the current time.
* Adding a Moon Dial will display the current moon phase,
* Adding a Map will display it. If there is more than one Map equipped, they can be cycled through by pressing C. Up to a maximum of three Maps can be inserted at the same time and any map from vanilla or Twilight Forest is supported.

### Aquatic Agility
This modifier combines the effects of Aqua Affinity and Respiration into a modifier and lets you add them to the Goggles.

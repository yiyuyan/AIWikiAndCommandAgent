Giant Leaves, like the other giant blocks, look like a much larger block of normal leaves.  Giant leaves are only found near the Cloud Cottage on top of a solid cloud over the Highlands, growing off of a Giant Tree. Due to the nature of the Giant Trees, the Giant Leaves are able to have their tops burn forever; however, unlike the Giant Log, Giant Leaves can be destroyed by a strong enough explosion. Giant Leaves can be crafted into 64 Leaves.

Skull Candle is a light-emitting decorative block made of a Skull and Candles that can also be worn as a helmet by players and mobs alike.

Skull Candles are made by right-clicking a head/skull block with a Candle. Up to four can be added, and it supports all sixteen vanilla Candle colors. Adding a candle to a Skull Candle can be done by right-clicking a compatible skull or head with a Candle, and a candle can be removed by crouching and right-clicking the Skull Candle. Only identical Candle colors can exist on the same Candle Skull. Below is a list of skulls or heads available that can be made.

When broken, Skull Candles will drop the skull or head and the number and color of candles that were placed on top. However, a tool enchanted with Silk Touch may be used to mine and obtain the Skull Candle block with their respective candle count and colour.

## Light
Much like vanilla Candles, Skull Candles will emit light based on the amount of Candles currently placed on top of the Skull/Head. Skull Candles are not lit when placed down and must be lit with a Flint and Steel, a Bow with the Flame enchantment, or a Fire Charge.

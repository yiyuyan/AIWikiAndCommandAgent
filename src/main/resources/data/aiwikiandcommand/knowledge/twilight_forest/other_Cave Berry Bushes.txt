Cave Berry Bushes are the colloquial term for berry bushes that can be found scattered throughout the caves of Twilight Forest. A Cave Berry Bush has 4 growth stages, where the last stage bears fruit. Once a berry bush has been harvested, it will slowly regrow more berries.

In order for a Cave Berry Bush to grow, it will need to be planted on a stone surface such as Stone, Netherrack, or other Ore blocks.

These bushes are slightly dangerous as walking into a Cave Berry Bush will deal 1 ) damage.

## Iron Oreberry Bush
【方块信息】
hardness：0.4
tool：Any Tool
luminance：0

Iron Oreberry Bush is a block that grows underground in caves. It can be used to obtain Iron Oreberries.

In order for an Iron Oreberry Bush to survive, it must be in light levels below 13, and will only grow in light levels below 10.

## Essence Berry Bush
【方块信息】
hardness：0.4
tool：Any Tool
luminance：0

Essence Berry Bush is a block that grows underground in caves under the Enchanted Forest. It can be used to obtain Concentrated Essence Berries.

Unlike other Cave Berry bushes, Essence Berry Bushes can survive and grow in any light level.

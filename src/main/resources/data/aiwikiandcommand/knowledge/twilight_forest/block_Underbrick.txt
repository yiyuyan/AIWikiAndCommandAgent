Underbrick is a block group which exclusively generates within the Goblin Knight Stronghold. Their name is quite apt, as they share appearances with that of Brick, but with a deeper colour toning to it, and are found buried underneath the surface of the Dark Forest

__TOC__

## Obtaining

### Underbrick
Underbrick is the most common variant from this block family, which is findable all throughout the Goblin Knight Stronghold.

### Cracked Underbrick
Cracked Underbrick is a variant of Underbrick that generates in the Goblin Knight Stronghold. The brick layering for this block differs from Underbrick and appears to be cracked in some parts.

### Mossy Underbrick
Mossy Underbrick is a variant of Underbrick that generates in the Goblin Knight Stronghold. The brick layering for this block differs from Underbrick and has some much greener bricks.

### Underbrick Floor
Underbrick Floor is a variant of Underbrick that does not generates in the Goblin Knight Stronghold. The brick is has a noticeably smooth face, similar to Encased Towerwood, and can be made by Stonecutting regular Underbrick.

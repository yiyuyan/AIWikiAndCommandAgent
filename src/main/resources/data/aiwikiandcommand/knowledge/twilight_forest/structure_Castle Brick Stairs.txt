There exists blocks in the shape of stairs with the same variants of Castle Brick or Castle Brick Pillar. Currently, these blocks are not implemented in the Final Castle.

## Castle Brick Stairs
【方块信息】
hardness：100
tool：Diamond Pickaxe

Castle Brick Stairs is a type of Castle Stairs that resembles Castle Bricks. These blocks look identical to Castle Brick, but in the shape of Stairs. At the moment, Castle Brick Stairs does not generate, but it can be crafted.

## Bold Castle Brick Stairs
【方块信息】
hardness：100

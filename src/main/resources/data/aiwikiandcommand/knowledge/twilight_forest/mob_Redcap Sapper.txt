Redcap Sappers are tougher versions of their cousins, the Redcap Goblins. Redcap Sappers can only be found in medium and large Hollow Hills, though they seem to only appear in spawners in medium hills. In addition to having more health, the Redcap Sappers could also drop TNT and blow you up! Unlike their Redcap cousins, they packed more than a single block of TNT, so be prepared for a bit of an explosive fight.

Like Redcap Goblins, they also drop Coal and their  Ironwood Pick and  Ironwood Boots, though they may be damaged.

【物品信息】
durability：9

The Zombie Scepter is a scepter dropped by the Twilight Lich which summons a temporary Loyal Zombie on each use.

Summoned Zombies will follow the owner around, attacking targets that are hit by the owner, attack the owner, or are classified as a monster. When summoned, a Loyal Zombie will be granted temporary Strength, but once this effect depletes, the Loyal Zombie will vanish.

The Zombie Scepter holds 9 charges and can be recharged by placing an empty Scepter of Life Draining with Rotten Flesh and a Potion of Strength, or an Exanimate Essence in a crafting table. The Scepter will never break once it's depleted. It can only be enchanted with Renewal.

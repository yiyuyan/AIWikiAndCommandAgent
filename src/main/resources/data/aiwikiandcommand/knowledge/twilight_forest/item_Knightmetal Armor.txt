Knightmetal Armor is an armor set made from Knightmetal Ingots. It is similar to the Ironwood Armor set, but with stronger armor rating and an armor toughness rating of 1.

__NOTOC__

## Knightmetal Helmet

The Knightmetal Helmet is a part of the Knightmetal Armor set, used in the Helmet slot. Knightmetal Helmets do not come automatically enchanted when crafted. When worn, it has an armor rating of 3, similar to the Diamond Helmet.

### Crafting

## Knightmetal Chestplate

The Knightmetal Plate is a part of the Knightmetal Armor set, used in the Chest slot. Knightmetal Chestplates do not come automatically enchanted when crafted. When worn, it has an armor rating of 8, similar to the Diamond Chestplate.

### Crafting

## Knightmetal Greaves

The Knightmetal Greaves is a part of the Knightmetal Armor set, used in the Legs slot. Knightmetal Greaves do not come automatically enchanted when crafted. When worn, it has an armor rating of 6, similar to the Diamond Leggings.

### Crafting

## Knightmetal Boots

The Knightmetal Boots is a part of the Knightmetal Armor set, used in the Boots slot. Knightmetal Boots do not come automatically enchanted when crafted. When worn it has an armor rating of 3, similar to the Diamond Boots.

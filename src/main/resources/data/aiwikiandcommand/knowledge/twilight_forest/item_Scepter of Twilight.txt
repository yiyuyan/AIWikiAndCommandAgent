【物品信息】
durability：99

The Scepter of Twilight is a scepter dropped by the Twilight Lich which shoots a bolt of magic.

This scepter shoots projectiles identical to the Lich's projectiles whenever it is right-clicked. These particles appear similar to Ender Pearl with a blue-green particle trail following them. Each projectile deals 6 (3 x ♥) of damage, dealing magic damage and can break Lich and Scepter of Fortification shields.

The Scepter of Twilight holds 99 charges and can be recharged by placing an empty Scepter of Twilight with an Ender Pearl or an Exanimate Essence in a crafting table. Recharging will only restore 9 charges. The Scepter will never break once it's depleted. It can only be enchanted with Renewal.

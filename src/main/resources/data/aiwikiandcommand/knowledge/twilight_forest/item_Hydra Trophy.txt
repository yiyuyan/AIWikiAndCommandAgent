The Hydra Trophy is a decorative trophy that is dropped after slaying a Hydra. It's a smaller version of a Hydra's head and can be placed two different ways. If placed on a block, it will have a closed mouth and act as a normal Hydra head. If placed on a side of a block, it will have an open mouth. When using the Progression system, the Hydra Trophy is one of the usable trophies on the Trophy Pedestal in the Goblin Knight Stronghold.

Wrought Iron Fence is a block found within the Lich Tower. It is similar to how a Fence works, connecting to neighboring Wrought Iron Fences to create a larger shape.

When placed as a single block, the Wrought Iron Fence will take on a post shape. As more are connected in sequence, the Wrought Iron Fences will extend, creating a long sequence of bars. If the Wrought Iron Fence is two blocks tall, a small ornate pattern will form  in the bars. By holding Shift and right-clicking with a Wrought Iron Fence, a finial will cap the post. A post will be made if a finial is placed in the middle of a connection

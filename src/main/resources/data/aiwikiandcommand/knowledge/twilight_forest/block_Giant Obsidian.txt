hardness：204,800
tool：Giant Pickaxe

Giant Obsidian looks like normal obsidian, but 4x's larger. It is only found surrounding the central vault of the Troll Caves. Unlike the other giant blocks, it is near impossible to harvest these blocks without the Giant's Pickaxe.

Magic Trees are the product of saplings rarely found in Leaf Dungeons. These trees are unique in that they grow a special kind of block in the middle of the trunk, a Magic Tree Core. The effects of a Magic Tree Core can be toggled, where they will change appearance based on their active state.

However, a Magic Tree can only be functional with the Magic Tree Core. While most of a Magic Tree may be typically harvested, the Magic Tree Core will revert to a regular Magic Tree Log, rendering the function of a Magic Tree useless. Magic Trees will not drop saplings of itself if the leaves are broken down.

## Tree of Time
320px|link=|center

Tree of Time is a large, robust tree with a thick trunk and leaves fading between green and red. In the center of a Tree of Time is a Timewood Clock, capable of increasing the rate crops grow and speeding up the function of Block Entities with functions relying on time.

Tree of Time consists of Timewood Logs and Timewood Leaves.

## Tree of Transformation
320px|link=|center

Tree of Transformation is a tall tree that resembles a shorter version of Canopy Trees but with grey logs and blue leaves that emit enchantment particles. In the center of a Tree of Transformation is a Heart of Transformation, capable of turning the surrounding biome into an Enchanted Forest.

Tree of Transformation consists of Transwood Logs and Transwood Leaves.

## Miner's Tree
320px|link=|center

Miner's Tree is a peculiar tree in the shape of a Pickaxe, with leaves fading between green and gold. In the center of a Miner's Tree is a Mining Core, capable of unearthing ores from the surrounding area in similar fashion to an Ore Magnet. Ores such as Coal Ore are ignored by this tree.

Miner's Tree consists of Minewood Logs and Minewood Leaves.

## Sorting Tree
320px|link=|center

Sorting Tree is a small, short tree with leaves that fade between light and dark green. In the center of a Sorting Tree is a Sortingwood Engine, capable of taking items within a small radius around the core and sorting them into all kinds of inventories in the wider radius.

Sorting Tree consists of Sortwood Logs and Sortwood Leaves.

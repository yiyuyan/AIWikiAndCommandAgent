Traveller's Wings is a piece of Traveller's Gear that can be worn in the Leg slot. Unlike most equipment, Traveller's Wings will not break when its durability is completely reduced. Instead, the ability and modifiers are rendered useless and it will no longer have any armor value.

When equipped, the Traveller's Wings comes with the High Jump ability, which allows the wearer to jump higher.

As the Traveller's Wings requires Gold Ingots in the crafting recipe, wearing Traveller's Wings prevents Piglin hostility.

## Modifiers
There are 6 additional modifiers that can be crafted into Traveller's Wings.

### Swap Hotbar
This modifier has two different purposes. Firstly, it combines the Traveller's Belt with the Traveller's Wings, visually displaying the two pieces of equipment as one when worn. Secondly, since the Traveller's Belt becomes a part of the Traveller's Wings, it also provides the Belt's innate Swap Hotbar modifier.

### Auto-Repair
This modifier allows for slow self-repair. The repair process does not require any materials and happens over time. In addition, repairing is sped up in direct sunlight, allowing for the gear to last longer. However, there is a 10 second cooldown for any possibility of self-repair after the armor piece has taken any durability damage which can lead to rapid decline in combat.

### Gradual Glide
This modifier allows for the wearer to slow down their descent from a fall. By crouching while in the air, the wearer will slow their falling speed. Gradual Glide can be automatically enabled with a config option, enabling Gradual Glide as soon as the wearer starts to fall.

### Double Jump
This modifier allows for the wearer to perform a second jump. While in the air after jumping, performing a second jump will give extra air time.

### Agile Ranger
This modifier lets the wearer move at walking speed when a ranged weapon such as a Bow is being pulled.

### Sidestep
This modifier adds the ability to dash to the left or right. By double-tapping the left or right movement key, the wearer will hop in the respective direction. Once a sidestep is performed, there will be a small cooldown before another sidestep can be performed.

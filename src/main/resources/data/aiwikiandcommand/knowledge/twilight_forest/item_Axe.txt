Twilight Forest's Axes, much like axe, can be used for harvesting blocks, have a high damage output as a weapon, and take the same Enchanting as vanilla axes. Just like Vanilla Axes, using any axe as a weapon will lower it's durability by 2 points.

## Ironwood Axe

The Ironwood Axe is an axe made from Ironwood Ingots. When crafted, the enchantment Fortune I will be automatically enchanted. If used as a weapon, the axe has an attack damage of 9 (4.5x♥).

### Crafting

## Steeleaf Axe

The Steeleaf Axe is an axe made from Steeleaf. When crafted, the enchantment Efficiency II will be automatically enchanted. If used as a weapon, the axe has an attack damage of 10 (5x♥).

### Crafting

## Knightmetal Axe

The Knightmetal Axe is an axe made from Knightmetal Ingots. The Knightmetal Axe has an attack damage of 10 (5x♥), with damage increased by 2 when used against targets without armor. Note that some mobs may not normally receive this extra damage due to their innate natural armor value (Such as Zombies).

### Crafting

## Golden Minotaur Axe

The Golden Minotaur Axe is an axe that is rarely dropped when a Minotaur is wielding one and is slain. In combat use, it deals 7 health (3.5x♥) of damage. When it is used while sprinting or "charging," 14 health (7x♥) is dealt instead. When used to strike a critical hit, it will only deal 10.5 (5.25x♥) damage, making the charging attack more effective for damage. It can be repaired in an anvil with a Gold Ingot or another Golden Minotaur Axe.

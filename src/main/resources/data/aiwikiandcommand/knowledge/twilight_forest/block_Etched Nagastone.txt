Etched Nagastone is a special type of block found at the Naga Courtyard. This block has a swirl pattern along the sides, and the top and bottom of the block has a 2x2 tile texture. In general, this block is similar to Logs, with the orientation changing depending on how the block is placed. Etched Nagastone can be used to craft Nagastone Stairs.

A Fiery Ingot is an item crafted from one Iron Ingot and one vial of either Fiery Blood or Fiery Tears. Fiery Ingots contain a magical property that causes items crafted using the ingots to acquire fiery properties. Fiery Ingots can be used to make a Fiery Sword, a Fiery Pickaxe, or Fiery Armor. As a means of storing away extra Fiery Ingots, one can also use nine to create a Block of Fiery Metal.

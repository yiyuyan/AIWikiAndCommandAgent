Sometimes Roots form strange, ore-like clusters, called Liveroots. Liveroots appear as roots webbed with a bright yellow-green color. They typically appear in clusters of between 4 and 8 blocks and can be found anywhere underground in the Twilight Forest. Breaking Liveroots without silk touch gives Liveroot, which forms a key ingredient in the creation of Raw Ironwood. Alternatively, "stripping" Liveroots will provide a Liveroot, leaving the root alone.

## Composting
Placing Liveroots into a Composter has a 65% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

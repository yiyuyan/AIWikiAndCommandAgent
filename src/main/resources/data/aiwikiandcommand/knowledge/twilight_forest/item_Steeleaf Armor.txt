Steeleaf Armor is armor made from Steeleaf. This armor set has higher defense than Ironwood Armor, but has less durability and comes pre-enchanted with some different enchantments.

__NOTOC__

## Steeleaf Helmet

The Steeleaf Helmet is a part of the Steeleaf Armor set, used in the Helmet slot. When crafted, Steeleaf Helmet is automatically enchanted with the Projectile Protection II enchant. When worn, it has an armor rating of 3, similar to the Diamond Helmet.

### Crafting

## Steeleaf Chestplate

The Steeleaf Chestplate is a part of the Steeelaf Armor set, used in the Chest slot. When crafted, Steeleaf Chestplate is automatically enchanted with the Blast Protection II enchant. When worn, it has an armor rating of 8, similar to the Diamond Chestplate.

### Crafting

## Steeleaf Leggings

The Steeleaf Leggings is a part of the Steeleaf Armor set, used in the Legs slot. When crafted, Steeleaf Leggings is automatically enchanted with the Fire Protection II enchant. When worn, it has an armor rating of 6, similar to the Diamond Leggings.

### Crafting

## Steeleaf Boots

The Steeleaf Boots is a part of the Steeleaf Armor set, used in the Boots slot. When crafted, Steeleaf Boots is automatically enchanted with the Feather Falling II enchant. When worn it has an armor rating of 3, similar to the Diamond Boots.

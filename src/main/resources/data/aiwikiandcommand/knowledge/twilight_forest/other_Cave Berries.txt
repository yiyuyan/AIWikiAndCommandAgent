Cave Berries are the colloquial term for berries that can be found scattered throughout Twilight Forest in the caves. Unlike Overworld Berries or Nether Berries, Cave Berries cannot be consumed.

## Iron Oreberry

Iron Oreberry is an item obtained from Iron Oreberry Bushes. It can be used to obtain Iron Nugget through smelting.

## Copper Oreberry

## Concentrated Essence Berry

Concentrated Essence Berry is an item obtained from Essence Berry Bushes. Using one will grant 6-20 XP to the user.

【生物信息】
类型：Hostile
生命值：25 (♥ x 12.5)
攻击力：Melee: 4 (♥♥)
Fire breath: 2 (♥ + fire damage)
掉落：Gunpowder (0-2)
ID：fire_beetle

Fire Beetles are a dangerous type of Beetle that can be found in Hollow Hills and Labyrinths. Fire Beetles can attack from a distance by spitting fire. Fire Beetles drop Gunpowder when killed.

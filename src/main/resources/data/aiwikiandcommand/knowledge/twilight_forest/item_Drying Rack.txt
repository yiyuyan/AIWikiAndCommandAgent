The Drying Rack is a functional block used for crafting. Items that are placed on a Drying Rack will slowly dry out, creating new items. These can range from Jerky to Gelatinous Slime Drops, but other items can be used.

Drying Racks can be crafted into a variety of wood types from vanilla to Twilight Forest.

## Crafting

Different wood types will craft into different Drying Racks. See  Data Values for all variations of Drying Rack.

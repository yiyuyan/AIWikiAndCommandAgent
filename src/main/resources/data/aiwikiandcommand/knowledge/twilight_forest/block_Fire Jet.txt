Fire Jet is a block often found in the Fire Swamp. These blocks appear to blend into their surroundings, but unlike the Smoker, these blocks will not emit any particles on a frequent basis. The danger from this block comes from the function. After the Fire Jet has made a popping sound a few times, it will erupt a tall column of fire which burns all entities in the stream of fire. Fire Jet can only be harvested with a Shovel, and can be used to craft Encased Fire Jets.

Fire Jets have three states. The first one is the Idle state, where the Fire Jet will not erupt any fire. If there is a Lava source under the block, the Fire Jet will enter a Popping state, consuming the Lava beneath the block and start emitting embers. Once enough time as passed, the Fire Jet will automatically enter the Flame state, where a large stream of fire erupts from the jet, also changing light value to 15. Finally, once the eruption stops, the Fire Jet will enter the Idle state, resuming the cycle if a Lava source is underneath the block.

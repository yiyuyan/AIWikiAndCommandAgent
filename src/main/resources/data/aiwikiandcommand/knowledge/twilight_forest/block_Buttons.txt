Buttons are a functional block crafted from Planks made from Twilight Forest trees. Much like a standard Button, these buttons are functionally similar to a wooden Button. These buttons can be activated by a shot Arrow, and will remain active when pressed for 30 ticks, or 1 and a half seconds.

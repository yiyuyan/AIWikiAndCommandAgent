Ghast Trap blocks are a type of Dark Tower Device found at the very top of the Dark Tower. The Ur-Ghast will spawn its minions, which are weaker versions of Carminite Ghastlings, around these traps. Killing a Carminite Ghastling will cause red particles to descend into the Ghast trap, charging it up. For every Ghastling you kill, the Ghast Traps will become louder and play more musical notes until it begins to smoke. When it's smoking, if activated by Redstone it will shoot out a red laser. This will suck up nearby Carminite Ghastlings and Ghastguards, killing them. It will not kill the Ur-Ghast instantly, but it will do critical damage.

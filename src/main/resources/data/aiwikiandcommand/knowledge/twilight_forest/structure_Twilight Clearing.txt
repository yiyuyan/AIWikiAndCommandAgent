Occasionally, explorers have found large clearings in the normally universal covering of trees in the Twilight Forest, strangely devoid of [Canopy Tree]]s. Referred to as Twilight Clearings these areas are a little flatter in topography compared to the greater Forest, with only the occasional Robust Twilight Oak Trees to break up the landscape. The open space would appear to be ideal for a base of operations, while those with the means to climb to the top of the giant oaks would have an excellent view to go with their new abode.

These clearings appear to only cover a small area in comparison to other biomes in the Forest, with only Hollow Hills having been observed with any regularity. Naga Courtyards and Lich Towers have been spotted on occasion, so approach these with caution.

No barriers have ever been encountered when exploring these areas - no bosses need to be defeated to travel through the clearings.

The Traveller's Gloves is a piece of Traveller's Gear that can be worn in the Chest slot.

Traveller's Gloves are cosmetic and have no additional functions. It can be crafted into Traveller's Vest, combining the appearance of the two into one slot. This process does not take up a modifier slot on the Traveller's Vest.

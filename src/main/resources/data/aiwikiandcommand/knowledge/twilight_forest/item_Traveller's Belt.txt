The Traveller's Belt is a piece of Traveller's Gear that can be inserted into the Legs slot.

The Traveller's Belt comes with the Swap Hotbar ability, which allows you to swap between your hotbar and an additional hotbar stored in the Traveller's Belt. By pressing V, the second hotbar stored in the Traveller's Belt swaps with your current hotbar's items. Unlike other Traveller's Gear, the Traveller's Belt cannot have modifiers added to it.

It can be crafted into Traveller's Wings, adding the Swap Hotbar ability from the Traveller's Belt as a modifier to the Traveller's Wings.

## Crafting

This recipe accepts all Chests.

Jerky is another form of food that can be obtained from the Drying Rack. This form of food processing is a cheaper way to create food from uncooked food items, restoring less hunger in exchange for more saturation. This process requires leaving a raw food item on the Drying Rack for about 5 minutes.

## Beef Jerky

Beef Jerky is a food item crafted from Raw Beef. It can restore 4 (2 x ) points of hunger and has a saturation modifier of 1.18.

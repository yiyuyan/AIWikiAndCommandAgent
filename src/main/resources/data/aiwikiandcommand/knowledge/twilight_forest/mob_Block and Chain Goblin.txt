Block and Chain Goblin is a monster that spawns in the Goblin Knight Stronghold. This goblin is very dangerous, due to the fact that it uses a spike block that it carries to use an attack that throws adventurers up into the air. This spike block also damages any entities that is touching it, regardless of whether it's being thrown or not.

Block-and-Chain Goblins drop Armor Shards when slain.

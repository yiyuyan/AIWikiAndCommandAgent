The Peacock Feather Fan is a unique item in the Twilight Forest that is found occasionally as treasure in Lich Towers and Medium Hollow Hills. When the Peacock Feather Fan is used, entities in front of the player will be pushed backwards. This applies to mobs, dropped items, and other players. Additionally, the strong winds from the fan may uproot flowers and grass. If the player is running and then jumps while simultaneously using the Peacock Feather Fan, a high-jump effect is produced.

The Peacock Feather Fan can be Enchanting with Unbreaking and Mending. It can only be enchanted by applying enchanted books on an Anvil.

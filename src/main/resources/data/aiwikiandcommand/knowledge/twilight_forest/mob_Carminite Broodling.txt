Carminite Broodlings are tiny monsters that scurry around in Dark Towers. They are much like a stronger Swarm Spider, so if you see them in groups, be prepared for battling them. Like Swarm Spiders and regular Spiders, Carminite Broodlings can rarely be ridden by a Skeleton, and drop Spider Eyes and String when killed.

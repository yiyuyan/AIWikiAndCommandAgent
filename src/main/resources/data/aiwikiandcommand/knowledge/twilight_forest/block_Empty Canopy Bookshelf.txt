Empty Canopy Bookshelves are decorative blocks that look similar to Chiseled Bookshelf, but they function very differently. These are the reminance of Death Tome Spawners, making this the only natural way that these shelves can appear.

## Obtaining
These bookshelves can only be made by the player by crafting.

类型：Hostile

攻击力：4 (♥♥) + poison
掉落：Bones (0-2)
Torchberries (0-2)
Golden Hoe (0-1)
ID：skeleton_druid

All that remains of an ancient order, the Skeleton Druids of the Twilight Forest seem harnessed to spawners inside Druid Huts, but it is possible to meet one in the Dark Forest riding a King Spider. Sometimes when a Hedge Spider or Swarm Spider spawns, a baby Skeleton Druid may ride it to form a Spider Jockey.

They attack with glittering spells that poison living creatures, and occasionally turn the blocks they hit into leaves. They may also apply a Bonemeal effect on grass, sprouting tall grass.

Killing a Skeleton Druid often yields Bones and Torchberries and occasionally, their Golden Hoe. Skeleton Druids are like normal Skeletons, and they can burn in direct sunlight, but due to the darkness in the Twilight Forest, they will not burn there.

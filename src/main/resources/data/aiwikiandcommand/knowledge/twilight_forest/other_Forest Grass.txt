Forest Grass is a plant that randomly generates throughout the Twilight Forest dimension. This plant is identical to Grass, sometimes dropping Seeds when broken and can be harvested with shears, the only difference being slightly taller than Grass and does not drop in darkness. However, harvesting Forest Grass will only result in Grass. For unknown reasons, perhaps magic, Forest Grass cannot be burned.

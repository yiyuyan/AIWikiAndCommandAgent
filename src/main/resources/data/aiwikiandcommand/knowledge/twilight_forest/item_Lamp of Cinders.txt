The Lamp of Cinders is a tool found in the central vault of the Troll Caves.  The Giant's Pickaxe is needed to open the vault. The Lamp is used to burn through the Thorns filling the Thornlands biome.  The lamp can be used in two different ways.  It can burn individual blocks by clicking on them.  Alternatively, you can hold the button, and "charge" the lamp to burn an entire area. The Lamp of Cinders has no limit on how many times the user can use it.

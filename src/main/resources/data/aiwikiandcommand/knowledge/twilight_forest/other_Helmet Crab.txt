The Helmet Crab is a small monster that spawns in the Goblin Knight Stronghold. Somewhat small and harmless-looking, this crustacean will use its pincers to fight curious adventurers. Helmet Crabs drop Armor Shards and Raw Cod when slain, and can drop Cooked Cod if killed by fire.

Raw Meef is an extremely common food item found only within the Labyrinth, that can be consumed raw or cooked into Meef Steak. Raw Meef can be obtained from slaying Minotaurs, dropping 0 to 1 meef on death. If a Minotaur is slain while on fire, it will instead drop Meef Steak! It's Meef! (Beef! Minotaur Beef! Get it? Huh? Huh!?) When eaten, Raw Meef will only provide the player with a lousy 2 () points of Hunger and 1.2 points of Saturation, but still just enough to be worth eating when in a tight spot.

If all other food sources have been eaten, the player will be rewarded with the Advancement: We Dine At Eternal Sundown

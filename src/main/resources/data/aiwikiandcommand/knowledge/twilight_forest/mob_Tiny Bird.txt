类型：Passive
生命值：1 ({{hh}})
攻击力：None
掉落：Feather (0-2)
ID：tiny_bird

Tiny Birds have 4 different feather patterns that resemble bluebirds, cardinals, goldfinches and sparrows. They are attracted to seeds, but there is no way to breed them. Since they are fairly small they get startled by the player, causing them to fly away, but then land on the ground shortly after. Tiny Birds occasionally drop Feathers when killed.

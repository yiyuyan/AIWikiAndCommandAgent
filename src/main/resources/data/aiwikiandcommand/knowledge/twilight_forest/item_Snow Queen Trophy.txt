The Snow Queen Trophy is a decorative trophy that is dropped after slaying the Snow Queen. It's a smaller version of the Snow Queen's head. In the Progression system, the Snow Queen Trophy is one of the trophies that can be used on a Trophy Pedestal in the Goblin Knight Stronghold.

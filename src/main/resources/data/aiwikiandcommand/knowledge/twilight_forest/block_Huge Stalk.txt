Huge Stalks are the result of Magic Beans when planted in a patch of Uberous Soil, forming a gigantic beanstalk that spirals upwards into the heavens. As such, these massive growths prove to be an excellent method of ascending to the Cloud Cottages that hover over the Twilight Highlands.

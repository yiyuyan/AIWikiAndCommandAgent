Mist Wolf is a hostile mob that spawns in the Dark Forest. These monsters hide in the darkness, disappearing in low light levels but revealing themselves the brighter the light level is. Mist Wolves are twice the size and toughness of an Overworld Wolf. In low light levels, attacks from the Mist Wolf can inflict Blindness, with the duration of the effect lasting longer in higher difficulties.

Mist Wolves do not drop anything on death. As the Mist Wolf shares a loot table with Wolf, any changes made to the Wolf drops will also apply to Mist Wolf.

The Ur-Ghast Trophy appears as a smaller version of the Ur-Ghast. This animated trophy that can be received in a treasure chest after slaying the Ur-Ghast. Unlike the other Boss Trophies, the Ur-Ghast Trophy appears as an animated, full-body version of the Ur-Ghast. Like the other Boss Trophies, the Ur-Ghast Trophy can be placed on any block. Although this trophy is usable on a Trophy Pedestal, it cannot be used on the pedestal if following the Progression system until at least one Goblin Knight Stronghold is conquered and the Ur-Ghast has been slain.
